## In one paragraph

**The books read, the state is neutral, the pump is off — and the bill for the state's lateness is now a number nobody pays.** Under the expected cost the state's deliveries net $\Omega$ about $-0.7$ a delivery at every split, two percent of $\bar B$ — neutral within $b_T$'s lag — and the bank is never asked: $\Omega$'s retained fees fund every summons, loans and debt read zero from the first period to the last, the wall never binds (Hypothesis 1 holds, as arithmetic said it must where the prior is the driver's rate). The *at cost* control under the bank is a different animal from run 12's: the bleed becomes debt, the debt crosses the wall, the state retracts (1,900–10,600 deliveries refused for credit a run), rebates pile up as liabilities of 2,400–8,800, on time falls to 0.79–0.89, and the requesters hoard what the state would not serve — the rule as written, and the burn rule it indicts. The index per period changes nothing in kind: P_t 31 / 33 / 36 / 36 against run 12's 31.6 / 34.1 / 37.4 / 37.3, honesty within noise. The red team finds the two-thirds limit exactly where §7.7 put it: at a half the automatic charge covers everything (b_T 0.52, the burn 8.4, no debt); at three quarters the burn is zero, the debt crosses the wall, the state retracts and its customers are owed 8,900 of rebates at the end — against the unbounded control's debt of 4,900 and rising; the rejecters drive b_T to 0.67, the limit, and make the state 63% of the market at a loss. The inflation test gives the quantity theory to the unit at the middle and the first **deflation** seen in any run at the bottom: issuance 5 / 10 / 20 a requester at 20 honest gives P_t 9.5 / 23 / 54 under the wallet rule (the strategy's constant predicts 11 / 23 / 47) — and real wealth by disposition is **not** invariant: the honest earn 790 real at issuance 5, 1,410 at 10, 1,310 at 20, because at low issuance the ratchet never starts and the members work at cost. Told: the withdrawing requester withdraws and reposts at every mark, gains nothing (rebates paid identical to the holder's), and honesty is unmoved. The pump: M grows by issuance alone (the flood's 74,000 against run 12's 99,400; the requesters' hoards 1,700 against 3,000), the field's capital sits on the loans line at 1,700–1,800 and comes back, the debt peaks at 400–500 and is repaid — and the lateness liability reaches **27,000 in the flood and 10,000 under the crowd, ages 400–500 ticks, and is not paid down within the run**: the state's whole fee income is a quarter of what it owes its customers for being late. **Two things to decide** (§8): the liability stock's scale, and what the state-rejecter red team can be.

## 2. The knobs (§8.8)

At 60/40, one at a time against the default (b_T prior 0.2, the wall one period): the prior 0.1 / 0.3 and the wall 2 / unbounded give **identical runs in every reading but one** — Ω retained 4,168 / 4,404 / 4,640 for the prior 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3. The prior is a transient: it sets the burn only until the state has closed ten deliveries, so it moves what the state keeps from its first dozen deliveries by a few hundred and nothing after. The wall's width does not matter where the wall is not approached. (The window of one or two periods was not run: b_T's rule — the last closed period if it closed ten, else back until ten — is the window, and at 60/40 the last period always closes seventy.)

## 7. The books (§8.7, Hypothesis 4; Q16)

In every cell the bank's position decomposes — minted by kind (issuance 60 a tick plus the genesis; emission 870–1,100 a run; the plants' pots 6,400–28,000 against 5,800–24,000 destroyed, the cost of truth 660–4,000 a run, rising with the state's share since the state's deliveries draw inspections and the dial's plants with them; the scenario 0), lent and repaid, outstanding, debt, liabilities — and I28's identity (lent $=$ repaid $+$ outstanding $+$ debt) and I30 held at every tick of the 600 runs with the goldens' random drive beside them. "The state never printed for itself" held as an invariant everywhere; the one creation that is not a loan or a rule is `mint_scenario`, unused here. Run 12's deficit re-read: at cost under the bank the 3,700–10,100 that 2.0.0 called "deficit" is loans outstanding of 965–1,495 at the peak (capital in flight, repaid), debt of 300–650 (losses, repaid from fees to a few hundred), and liabilities of 2,400–8,800 (the rebates the retracted state could not pay) — three numbers that say three different things.

## 8. For the decision

1. **The lateness liability's scale.** Under a riot the rebate stock reaches 10,000–27,000, ages 400–500 ticks, and is a quarter paid by the state's whole fee income; the rule sized the rebate at $r_s$ a tick capped at $B$ for a state that printed to pay it. Candidates, all §19 design decisions: cap the stock (a liability the state cannot pay within a period is forgiven, and said so); price the rebate from the state's income (the period's fees divided over the late requests); or leave it as the reading it now is — the honest bill for a full field — and let the busy-line mark do the work the rebate did. The text's "a liability stock that peaks at a few thousand" was off by an order of magnitude in the flood.
2. **What the state-rejecter red team can be.** §14 keeps the actor from the reviewer, so the cell was run as a declared non-conforming reading; it shows that a reviewer who could tell would drive b_T to the two-thirds limit and make the state most of the market at a loss. Either the view stays blind — and the red team is struck as impossible by construction — or 2.2.0 decides whether a reviewer may know it is inspecting the state's work.
3. **Hypothesis 5's second half.** Real wealth by disposition moves with issuance through the strategy's quantity constant (790 / 1,410 / 1,310); the text should say that printing is not neutral in distribution even where it is in work — or that the invariance was the hypothesis and it failed.
4. **The wall under the at-cost control** retracts the state chronically rather than letting it bleed; that is the design, and the control's figures (refusals 1,900–10,600, on time 0.79–0.89) are what "the burn rule is wrong" looks like in the record. Nothing to decide; recorded.
5. **For 2.2.0 (entry and delegation):** the inflation cells say the price is the quantity's and the quantity is the strategy's; entry is the lever, and under the bank the state's books are now clean enough that the supply side can be read against them.
