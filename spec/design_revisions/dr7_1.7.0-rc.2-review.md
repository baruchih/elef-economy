## Implementation readings, for the record

- **$R_T$ as capital held is the engine's 1.4.0 reading over two periods.** `R_T` with `reserve_demand=False` is already `max(troop_capital_peak, troop_capital_peak_prev)`, the running peak of `_troop_capital_now()` (costs purchased plus locks held, every troop in the field) over this period and the last, rolled at the period's end. The troop-account flag will set that reading; the 1.5.0 demand readings ($D_s$, its refused part, the period's demand peak) stay in the record as readings, since Q13 and Q14 still read them. I23 keeps "$R_T\ge$ capital held" and retires "$R_T\ge$ the last $D_s$" with the component it belonged to. `--rules 1.6.0` restores the demand reserve with the merged fund.
- **Hypothesis 3 will read a rising state's share, and that is not a preference.** In run 8's two-thirds cell at 60/40 the fund refused 1,979 case-ticks of award summonses and 314 of seat summonses for want of funds. With the surplus in Ω those refusals fall, and the state delivers more under the same summons rules. The share should be read with the refusals beside it: a rise matched by a fall in refusals is capacity; a rise without one would be the finding the guards exist for. The run-8 in-flight figures support §6's reserve: about 24 troop deliveries and one review seat in flight at that cell, roughly 480 of capital, against the text's "about 450".
- **Two wallets, one flag, and the tests behind it.** `TROOP_ACCT` beside `DUTY_FUND`; troop ids alias to the troop account; the carry check, the advance, its repayment, the lock reconciliation and the troops' capital move to it; the surcharge stays in the duty fund and the batch pays its whole unlocked balance as uplift; the troop account's unlocked balance above $R_T$ goes to Ω after the advance is repaid, at the batch, before step 5's order runs. The implementer's own `test_v14`–`test_v16` are pinned to their revisions' flags (merged fund, demand reserve, the half cap), as `test_v12`–`test_v13` already are; reviewer-authored tests are not touched.
- **The driver.** `--r-s` does not exist yet and is added for the pin; `--omega-rate` for the sweep; `--duty-cap` defaults to the rules' fraction (two thirds under 1.7.0, half under `--rules 1.6.0` and earlier) and stays its own knob, so §8.1's three cells are the flag alone, the cap alone, both; the income and outflow columns from the period record.
