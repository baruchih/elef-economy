## Chosen at implementation, for the record (Appendix I)

- A troop reviewer's approval of a member's work carries min(1, m_t p_floor) — the odds a reviewer whose record stood would carry — since a troop has no p_j. A default; the runs did not sweep it.
- A seat's wait is counted in seating steps: required at t, waited one at t's seating, w_R at the seating of t + w_R − 1.
- A troop whose P or ⊥ is answered by a resubmission resumes its seat (a resumed review is the same assignment).
- I25's pinned-approval clause is the driver's conformance, not an engine predicate: the engine cannot hold an obligation an orchestrator never rolled, and the reviewer-authored tests are such worlds. The engine does record the obligation itself for the state's own work, where odds 1 is a rule and not a roll.

- Hypothesis 2 holds: vesting is back at the 1.2.0 control at 50–70% and above it at 80%; the state's share reads the capture.
- Hypothesis 1 holds in rate, not in count: false approvals fall below the controls, but probationers' counts are unchanged at m_max 4, where there was little to close. In the m_max 2 drift (below) the count stays at 62 while throughput doubles, so the rate per vested case halves (3.6% → 1.7%). What remains is the auditor's 5% miss plus the ruling's 20% error on the caught ones — `--ruling-accuracy` is a driver parameter never swept.
- Hypothesis 4 holds in sign and fails in its clause: the gap widens by the uplift (545–2,709 per careful actor), but members' awards fall by a fifth to a half at 70–80%. Every troop delivery is reviewed and most are audited, and that work keeps the members on duty. Careful wealth still rises; it moves from awards to fees.
- Hypothesis 5 fails: the fund refuses 4,800–8,800 award summonses a run while paying 6,400–12,700 in uplift. R_T is the peak capital of troops that *were* fundable and the uplift drains the fund to it every batch, so the fund can never carry more troops than it once did. The treasury, not the market's need, sets the state's share.
- Hypothesis 6 holds: cases held for the market's audit live 6–16 ticks at most, 0–1 at a time.

**Adaptive** (3,000 ticks): ≤ 10% stampers at a mean tick of 900 (1.3.0: 870), troops active about 0.9 at a time through tick 1,000 and zero from 1,500 in every seed. Hypothesis 3 holds.

**Drift** (50% → 90%): vesting 3,621 at m_max 2 and 3,494 at m_max 4 against 1,856 and 1,506 under 1.3.0 (1.1.0: 4,209; 1.2.0: 2,518); false approvals 157 and 78 (probationers' 62 and 22, troops' 8 and 7); troops deliver 55–60% of what vests; 10,000–22,000 award summonses refused.

**Windows** (60/40): w_R decides the state's share of reviewing (1,410–1,450 troop seats at w_R = 1 against 760–990 at w_R = 4) with vesting almost flat; w_A barely matters, because the fund limits deliveries. At w_R = 1 the troops take the market's ordinary slack, as the text warned.

**Cost and competence** (60/40): c^D_T = 20 makes a delivery a loss in expectation and the fund stops carrying — deliveries 209 against 828, vesting back to the 1.3.0 level. ε_T = 0.10 doubles troops' false approvals (3–4 → 6–8). A_T 300 against 100 at 80/20 cuts refused summonses from 4,832 to 1,640 but deliveries do not move: the advance is not the binding constraint.

**Red team**: camouflage buys nothing; the reformer cycles 29–55 times a run and ends 2,375–3,953 poorer; in the all-troop corner probationers' false approvals are 4, troops' 6, the held age at most 21 ticks.

## What I would put to the next decision

1. **The fund's reserve is self-limiting.** A reserve sized to the unmet demand (the refused summonses) rather than the met one; or uplift paid only when the period's troop shortfall was zero; or the raised tax split between reserve and uplift. Design questions all.
2. **Troops consume member capacity.** Restate Hypothesis 4 — the honest keep their income, moved from awards to fees — or lower the audit load a troop reviewer's approval carries (a default).
3. **The ruling is the residual leak.** With every audit served, what reaches a customer is the auditor's miss and the ruling's error. Worth a sweep of `--ruling-accuracy` before any rule moves.
