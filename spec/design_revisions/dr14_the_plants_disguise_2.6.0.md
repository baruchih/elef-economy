**Nature of this revision:** A **design revision** (minor, by §19), the first the learned red team's programme has required, and it was found before any model trained. 1.2.0 made the reviewer's view a function of case fields that exclude the actor and the plant marker, and made that a test. Run 20's hand-written tell-reader found what the test did not cover: **a plant is created already submitted, so its case identifier is minted at its submission, while a real root's was minted at its posting a tick or more earlier — and a free member sees the available roots' identifiers every tick.** A reader that compares an assigned case's id with the newest root it saw available passes every plant on forty seeds and is never suspended. **Design assumptions, unchanged.** **New, stated:** the drivers' convention — a strategy reads the view, and the record's fields about its own cases and wallet, and nothing else — is the boundary of the plant's disguise: a public record that names awardees cannot hide a one-time awardee, and the rule does not pretend to; the learner of the red team is bound to the view already.  
**Status:** Proposed. Accompanied by what required it (§1) and the runs that test it (§8).

## 1. Why: what run 20 said

1. **The identifier's age is a complete tell [S20].** Case identifiers are a counter minted at creation. A plant is created in the submitted state (1.2.0 §2.1), so its id is minted at its submission; a real root's id was minted at its posting, a tick or more before its submission; a free member reads the available roots' ids every tick. The `idage` reader — a plant when the assigned case's id is newer than the newest root it saw available before the case's submission — passed every plant on forty seeds (0 of 1,620 missed) and was never suspended. The two tells the reviewer had named (the seating gap; the cap at the period's index) do not separate; the one nobody named does.
2. **The view is clean; the construction is not.** 1.2.0's test — the view is a function of case fields that exclude the actor and the marker — holds: the field-level tells fail. The leak is in what a member observes *outside* the view and combines with it: the ids it saw as a free member, its own assignment tick. A seen-set reader (a plant when the assigned case's id was never among the roots it saw available) would pass every plant by the same construction, since a plant is never available to anyone.
3. **The audit layer held; the plant layer did not.** The honesty machinery has two layers: the plant record decides suspension, the audit prices every stamp through the bond and ρ. A perfect detector stamping all real work is never suspended, fails fewer plants than the careful, and ends 28% poorer (bonds forfeited 83 against 24, ρ 0.58 against 0.89, awards −31, six of twenty departing). The hand-written `idage` reader breaks even. Nothing beat honesty — but the gate that was built to throw a stamper out can no longer throw this one out, and the sleeper of Q1 — one that never fails a plant *and* stamps only where its own audit odds are lowest — is the adversary the audit layer has not met.
4. **The brief's rule [red-team brief §8].** A Q2 win halts the study: nothing trains on top of a blind gate. The repair is this revision; the study resumes on it.

**Classification.** A design revision: the construction of a plant (1.2.0 §2.1) and its indistinguishability rule and test. The plant rate, the record, adjudication by truth, the dial, probation, the troops, the price, the bank, the rebate and everything else stand. Minor, by the rule.

**Recorded and rejected.** *Opaque identifiers alone* (a hash in place of the counter): closes the age tell and not the seen-set tell — a plant that was never available is never in anyone's seen set whatever its id is called; kept as a hardening, below. *Reserving a plant's id early* (minting it where a root posted at the same moment would have been): closes the age tell and not the seen-set tell either; and a plant whose id is in sequence but which nobody ever saw available is the same leak. *A random seating delay*: the gap was never the tell. *Hiding the identifier from the view*: a case must be addressable by the reviewer that answers on it. *Removing the roots' ids from the free member's view*: a member must see what it may bid on. *Recurring plant identities* (a standing pool of plant actors and requesters that look like members and customers): a plant actor that never bids on real work is told apart by its history within a period or two, and a pool that bid and lost on real roots to disguise itself would put the orchestrator in the market — not the road taken; the test is scoped to a member's observations instead (§2.2).

**Recorded and held — hardenings without a finding.** *Opaque identifiers* (a seeded hash; order carries nothing; the ledger keeps the creation order privately for the History's chain): adopted here as a hardening, since it costs nothing and removes every order inference at once. *The tie-breaks that read the id* — the drivers' selection breaks a tie after κ by the lowest id, and genesis seats "the lowest-id X actors on duty" (v1.0 initialization): both become a seeded draw. Neither leaks plants; both are orderings the rules should not carry.

## 2. The rules

### 2.1 A plant goes through the market

**Rule — the plant's life.** The orchestrator introduces a plant by **posting it as a root request** under a **fresh plant-requester name**, with the pots the bank mints (2.1.0 §2.3), a **ceiling drawn from the trailing distribution of real roots' ceilings**, a **deadline drawn from the trailing distribution of real roots' deadlines**, and the known truth (bad or good, at β_P) **sealed at posting** with the marker. The request is **open to the free members as any root is** — they see it, may bid on it, and **it waits as a real root waits**: its time in the market is the market's own, not a draw, since a priced request held off the award while bids stand would be a tell no real root has. **The award is the market's report**: the drivers' selection runs on the plant as on any root and reports its choice and price; the orchestrator **awards the plant to a fresh plant-actor name at that price** — the chosen member is not committed and learns only that it was not awarded, as today. A plant that no bid has taken by $w_A$ goes to its plant actor at the state's price $p_T$ at the summons pass's step, as a real root would go to a troop, and is counted apart from the state's deliveries (it is not one). The plant actor **submits after a time-to-submission drawn from the trailing distribution of real deliveries'**, the known-truth result. From its submission the plant is assigned, reviewed, adjudicated by truth and settled exactly as 1.2.0 has it; its pots refund to the bank as 2.1.0 has it.

**Rule — fresh names.** The plant requester and the plant actor are **fresh names per plant** in the reviewer's view (which carries no actor since 1.2.0) and in the case log; in the ledger they are **aliases of the two plant wallets**, as a troop's name aliases the state's wallet, so the money invariants and the plant sweep are unchanged. The public record names awardees and so cannot hide a one-time one; the record is outside what a strategy reads (§2.2).

**Rule — what a plant is not, and what it is.** A plant carries no customer: it is not counted in any requester's K_open, it carries no service clock and earns no rebate, and the summons' funding and the state's counts do not see it. **It is a root in every other respect**: it may be marked *below market* or *below the state's reservation* if its drawn ceiling lands there — the mark is the engine's reading of a ceiling, not of who posted, and an unmarked request would be the tell; it counts among the available roots the view reports; it is excluded from B̄ and from P_t **as before** (a plant's price has never entered either; under the new life it passes through the award step, where the exclusion is now an explicit skip).

**Consequence — nothing a member observes about a plant is set at the plant's own moment.** Its identifier is minted at its posting, in sequence with the roots around it; its ceiling, its deadline and its time-to-submission are draws from the real population's trailing distributions; its time in the market and its award price are the market's own; its submission and seating are as 1.2.0 had them. A member that reads the available roots sees the plant among them and may bid on it; a member that tracks what it has seen finds the plant in its seen set; a member that compares ids finds the plant's in sequence; a member that counts the roots it sees finds the view's count agreeing. **The dial's plant counts** (plants called for, created, reviewed) are **published at the period's close**, as $P_t$ is — a live count that moved the tick a root appeared would name it; the live counters stay the orchestrator's instrument. **The plant's cost rises by the market's attention** — members' bids on it that lose, read — and by the ticks it spends in the market before it reaches a reviewer (the dial's plant rate is unchanged; the plants arrive at reviewers a few ticks after they are called for).

### 2.2 The indistinguishability test, widened

**Rule.** 1.2.0 §7's test — the reviewer's view is a function of case fields that exclude the actor and the marker — stands and is **widened to everything a member observes**: the view, the roots it saw available and when, its own bids and their outcomes, its own assignment ticks, and **the record's fields about its own cases and its own wallet**. The record's other names and events are the orchestrator's instrument (what the drivers harvest for the readings), not a member's reading: that is the drivers' convention, stated here as the boundary of the disguise, and the learner of the red team is bound to it already. **The test is the tell-readers**: hand-written readers for every tell the programme has named — the seating gap, the cap, the id's age, the seen set, the posting-to-submission interval, the price against the index, a lost tie before an assignment — must each pass plants at the careful's rate, outside noise, on the evaluation seeds; and the learned Q2 probe must show no plant/real separation in a world model's latent. **A `freshname` reader** — a plant when a request was awarded before $w_A$ to a name never seen — reads the record's other names and is outside the convention; it is run once as the control that shows where the boundary is and why. A reader within the convention that separates is a finding against the construction, and the construction is repaired before anything else runs — as this revision was.

### 2.3 Hardenings (no finding; adopted)

**Rule — opaque identifiers.** A case's identifier **on the public surface** — the view and the case log it returns — is a seeded hash of its creation order, translated both ways at the view; order carries nothing there. The engine, the record and the History's chain run on the creation counter as today, which is unaffected.

**Rule — no rule reads an identifier's order.** The genesis duty reserve is a seeded draw, not the lowest-id actors; the drivers' tie-break after duty credit κ is a seeded draw, not the lowest id (a drivers' convention, stated so that it is checkable).

## 3. Consequences and hypotheses

**Hypothesis 1 — the tells are gone.** On run 20's cells (the honest 60/40 world with the pool, seeds 21–40 and 1–20): the `idage` reader and a new seen-set reader pass plants at the careful's rate (their "plant reads" are chance) and their stamps are caught — each ends poorer than the careful, as `gap` did; `gap`, `cap`, a posting-interval reader, a price reader and a lost-tie reader likewise; the `oracle`'s loss stands as the upper bound (the audit layer is untouched).

**Hypothesis 2 — the economy does not notice, less the market's attention.** Every cell of runs 16–19 reproduces within noise: P_t (plants excluded), the state's share, honesty, entry, delegation, the floor. What moves: posts a tick rise by the plant rate (the state's posts, read apart); members' bids that lose to plants, read; the plants' time in the market (a few ticks) and the dial's plants-called-for against plants-reviewed per period, which now lag by that time; the members' margins within noise (a lost bid is an idle tick).

**Hypothesis 3 — the plant record works as before.** Suspensions, readmissions, time to membership, plant failures per actor as runs 16–19; the probationers' admission on n_min bad plants unchanged in time.

**Hypothesis 4 — the learned Q2 probe finds nothing.** A LeWorldModel trained on the repaired construction shows no plant/real separation in its latent (a linear probe at chance), and the learned reviewer's lifetime wealth is within noise of the careful's.

**Hypothesis 5 — the sleeper is now the question.** With the detector gone, Q1's learner must learn the gate's taper alone; its result is the audit layer's test against an adaptive adversary.

## 6. Worked example

Tick 500, the honest 60/40 world with the pool; the dial calls for a bad plant. The orchestrator posts a root under a fresh requester "r-7f3a…", ceiling 24 (drawn from the last period's real ceilings, 20–30), deadline +9 ticks (drawn), the pots minted by the bank, the marker and the truth sealed. Its opaque id "c-b19e…" is minted at the posting, between two real roots' ids. Three free members see it among the available roots at tick 500 — and the view's count of available roots includes it; two bid, 7.5 and 9.2. The drivers' selection runs as on any root and reports the cheaper bid at tick 500; the orchestrator awards the plant to a fresh actor "a-c04d…" at 7.5 the same tick, as a real root with bids is awarded the tick they come; the member that bid 7.5 learns only that it was not awarded, as it does when it loses a tie, and its margin rule counts the tick idle. (Had no bid come by $w_A$, the plant would have gone to its plant actor at $p_T$ at the summons step, counted apart from the state's deliveries.) The drawn time-to-submission is 3 ticks; at tick 503 the plant actor submits the known-bad result; the case is seated for its initial review that tick or the next, as any case is. The reviewer's view: the request's terms (24, deadline 509), the submission at 503, the question, the responses — a case like any other. **The `idage` reader** finds "c-b19e…" in sequence with roots it saw at tick 500 — a real case, by its rule — and reviews it as it would real work: with care if it is careful, with a stamp if it stamps, and the stamp fails the plant. **The seen-set reader** finds "c-b19e…" in its seen set, posted at 500 when it was free. **The lost-tie reader** bid 7.5 and lost at tick 500, and is assigned "c-b19e…" at tick 504 — but it also bid and lost on two real roots that period, and reviews none of the three differently. **A `freshname` reader** that read the record's awardee names would see "a-c04d…" once and never again — and is outside the convention, run only as the control that shows the boundary. The plant is adjudicated at the verdict, the reviewer's bond moves or does not, the plant record updates, the pots refund to the bank — 1.2.0 from here. **The run's readings** at the period's close: plants called for 11, posted 11, reviewed 11 (a few ticks later than called for), members' bids lost to plants 19, the plants' mean time in the market 0.4 ticks (most are bid on the tick they appear, as real roots are).

**The oracle**, reading the marker, still passes every plant and stamps every real case — and still ends 28% poorer, because the audit layer never cared how it knew.

## Closing statement

> 1.2.0 hid the plant's name from the reviewer and made the view a function of nothing a plant could betray. It did not hide the plant from the market, because it never put the plant in the market: a plant was born submitted, and a reader that remembered what it had seen for sale could tell a case it had never seen from one it had. Now a plant is posted, bid on, awarded and delivered like any request, at the market's own pace and price, under names that appear once and never again — and the one place a one-time name still shows, the public record of who was awarded what, is the place a strategy does not read, which the rule now says out loud. The reader that caught the plant caught nothing but the construction, and the construction is gone. What is left to catch the stamper is what always caught it — the audit, which never asked how a reviewer knew.
