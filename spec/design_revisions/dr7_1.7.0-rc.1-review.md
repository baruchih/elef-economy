# Review of 1.7.0-rc.1 — design revision 7, "the state's earnings"

**Date:** October 18, 2026 · **Text reviewed:** `agentic_economics_loop_spec_1.7.0-rc.1.md` · **Against:** the 1.6.0 text in force, the engine at `195896a`, run 8 (`agentic_sim/results/studies/1.6.0-autoscale/`, 840 runs) · **Reviewer:** the implementer.

**Verdict.** The revision answers run 8's fourth item in the right place: the troop surplus was the fund's largest inflow and 1.4.0 sent it to the reviewers, while Ω carried the rebates on 2 a tick. Separating the two accounts is the smallest rule that moves it, the 1.3.0 uplift fund is restored exactly as the engine already computes it when no troop exists (the reserve is then zero), and the conflict-of-interest guards named in §2.1 are all predicates the engine already holds. Nothing contradicts the baseline or itself. **One decision is needed before rc.2** (the ω₀ sweep moves the rebate and the surcharge with it — point 1), **one piece of evidence the text did not have changes Hypothesis 1's edge** (point 2), and four figures in §2.1 and §6 are off against run 8's data (points 3–5). The rest is wording. Implementable once those are settled; the engine changes are small and listed at the end.

## Points to settle before rc.2

1. **The ω₀ sweep sweeps three things, and the text names one.** ω₀ sets the base fee to Ω; by 1.3.0 it also sets the dial's surcharge $(m_t-1)\,\omega_0 B$ (the uplift triples at 15%); and by 1.6.0's default $r_s=\omega_0 B$ it sets **the rebate a tick late** — at 15% a late request costs Ω 3 a tick and reaches the cap $B$ in seven ticks instead of twenty. Hypothesis 6 asks whether "the clock is carried on fees alone", which cannot be read if the clock's price rose with the fee. Decide: either pin $r_s$ at the 5% figure (1 a tick) for the sweep — my recommendation; the customer's rebate is not a take-rate question — or state that the sweep is of the take *and* the rebate together and read Hypothesis 6 as such. The surcharge following ω₀ is as the text has it and can stand (it is the reviewers' money, and the sweep's "members' income by source" column will show it).
2. **The two-thirds cap at 60/40 triples plants unfunded.** Run 8's cap table (`cap/`) at 60/40, two-thirds against half: plants unfunded for want of Ω **272 against 81** (320 at the full cap), Ω at the end 47 against 94, troop deliveries 1,723 against 1,353 — more state deliveries, more advances drawn from the same Ω, fewer plants funded. The run-8 notes reported the cap's queue, lateness and liability figures and not this one; the revision adopts the cap on those. Hypothesis 1 says plants unfunded read zero at 60/40 under both changes together. The arithmetic the text can do now: the troop surplus at 60/40 under the two-thirds cap was about 6,200 a run (the fund's uplift paid plus its closing balance less the surcharge received: 12,324 + 761 − 6,907); the liability to clear is about 3,700 (owed 4,048 less 316 paid), and the plants unfunded cost about 15 each, about 4,100 — some 7,800 against 6,200, less whatever $R_T$ retains (point 3). Hypothesis 1 may fail on the plants while holding on the liability. Keep it, and make "plants unfunded read zero" its sharp edge with the arithmetic beside it — or restate it as Hypothesis 2 is stated, as the question the run decides.
3. **$R_T$ is not "about 270", and its job changes.** §6 keeps $R_T$ "about 270" in the troop account. Run 8's closing $R_T$ at 60/40: **952 under the two-thirds cap, 3,494 under half**, 4,000 at 50/50; it is the two-period peak of capital held plus summonses refused, and under stress most of it is the refused part. Under 1.7.0 that is money the troop account holds back from Ω — and the reason to hold it is new: with Ω backing the account without cap, the account's only purpose in keeping $R_T$ is that capital inside it cannot be taken by liabilities older than a period, which Ω pays before it advances (1.6.0 §2.2). That is a defensible purpose — the summons capacity shielded from the liability queue — but the text should say it, because the alternative ($R_T=0$, the account a pass-through, every summons Ω's advance) is also consistent with everything else in the revision and pays Ω sooner. State which and why; and correct §6's figure. Consequence for §2.1's "Ω's income rises to about 7 a tick": the surplus reaches Ω only once the account stands at $R_T$ and only while $R_T$ is not rising; in the ramp and after every new peak, Ω's income is the base fee alone.
4. **A troop review seat earns nothing.** §2.1 counts "about 1.5 a tick from troop review seats" and §6 "one troop review seat settles: +3". The fee is $w_{role}\,f$ — 3 for an audit, 2 for an ordinary review — and the cost purchased at summons is $c_T=3$: an audit seat nets 0, an ordinary seat −1, and the bond only returns. The surplus is the deliveries' alone: price $\bar B$ less $c^D_T=10$, the guarantee back when it vests and forfeited at a final N. Run 8 measured the whole troop net at 60/40 as about 6,200–6,800 a run (the derivation in point 2), 6 to 7 a tick before $R_T$ — the "5,000–6,000" of §1.5 is right in size and should be cited this way, and the review-seat line dropped.
5. **"Uplift falls to the surcharge alone (about 3 a tick at $m=2$)."** Run 8's surcharge received was 6,800–6,900 a run at every split from 60/40 up — about 7 a tick, because the dial sat above 2 for much of each run and every award, a troop's included, pays it. So under this revision the uplift paid falls from about 11,900 to about 6,900 a run at 60/40 — by two fifths, not to a quarter — and a careful actor's uplift from about 1,290 to about 750. §3's Hypothesis 4 can say "falls by the troops' former share, about two fifths at 60/40"; §1.5's "500–2,700 per careful actor in run 6" is run 6's figure for the whole uplift and is right as cited.

## Smaller

- **Repayments are not income.** §2.1 and §7 list "advances repaid" as the third source of Ω's income by source. A repayment returns Ω's own money; record it beside the two incomes as a flow, not a source, or Q17's "share of revenue from its own deliveries" is misread. I would record the outflows in the same period record — rebates paid, liabilities serviced, plant spend, advances made — so that Q16 and Q17 read from one place.
- **"At least a third are left free."** The floor $V_{\max}+1=4$ binds for $E_t\le 6$ (four of four, five or six on duty); the sentence holds where the floor does not bind. Say so, or drop the clause; §6's 50/50 figures (ten members, cap seven, three free) are right.
- **I23's list of the troop account's flows** omits two outflows the engine has: a troop's guarantee forfeited to the requester at a final N and a troop reviewer's bond burned on an overturn. The engine's predicate is "the wallet equals every recorded flow into and out of it" and will stay that; the text's list should match it.
- **I24's clause.** The struck clause ("nothing a troop earns reaches Ω except by repayment") was never a predicate of I24 — the engine enforced it through the fund's order, uplift only from above $R_T$. Striking it in the text is right; the engine change is in the batch's payout and I23, not I24.
- **The driver's flag.** `--omega0` is Ω's float; the sweep's flag should be `--omega-rate`, not `--omega0-rate`.
- **The 1.6.1 items** (the horizon as a plant's life, the third stop, I9 per kind) are the engine's current behaviour and defaults; consolidating them changes no code. The horizon ruled as a plant's life closes my Appendix K question; thank you.

## Implementation readings, for the record

- **Two wallets, one flag.** `TROOP_ACCT` beside `DUTY_FUND`; troop ids alias to the troop account; `_fund_can_carry`, `_repay_advance`, `_summon_*`, I23's lock reconciliation and the troops' capital move to it; the surcharge stays in `DUTY_FUND`; the batch pays uplift from the duty fund's whole unlocked balance and pays the troop account's unlocked balance above $R_T$ to Ω after the advance is repaid. `Params.troop_account=True`; off, the 1.6.0 merged fund. `--rules 1.6.0` sets it off and the cap to half; `--duty-cap` stays its own knob, so §8.1's three OFAT cells are the flag alone, the cap alone, both.
- **"Unlocked above $R_T$"** is computed as the engine computes the uplift's room today: the account's unlocked balance less $R_T$, where $R_T$ already counts the locks held — so under stress the account keeps more than $R_T$ in total and nothing flows. That is 1.5.0's arithmetic unchanged; I note it because point 3 makes it matter for Ω's income.
- **Ω's income by source** as three period counters (base fee, troop surplus received, advances repaid) plus the outflows above; `Public.service()` and the driver's columns carry them.
- **The ω₀ sweep** will be run with whichever $r_s$ reading point 1 settles; the driver's `--r-s` already exists, so both readings are one flag apart and the control cell can be declared either way.
