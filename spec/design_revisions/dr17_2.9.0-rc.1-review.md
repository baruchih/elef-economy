# Review of 2.9.0-rc.1 — design revision 17, "the plant takes the wrong step"

**Date:** October 27, 2026 · **Text reviewed:** `agentic_economics_loop_spec_2.9.0-rc.1.md` · **Against:** the 2.8.0 text in force, the engine at `e32a8db9` (the view's conformance pass landed), runs 36 and 37 (notes §54–§63), the reviewer's nine points and the ruling on `audit_odds_at_verdict` (October 27) · **Reviewer:** the implementer. Nothing of 2.9.0 is built.

**Verdict. Implementable with three corrections to the rules as written, four corrections of fact and ten readings.** The content — the plant takes the honest steps at the real rate and the wrong steps at the orchestrator's — is the right reading of runs 36 and 37: every seat a case reaches only through someone's dishonesty was priced only as much as the real population was dishonest, and no rate of honest-shaped plants moved the audit seat. The planted approval sends the orchestrator's known truth to the audit seat at a rate the population does not set; the planted lie does the same for the panels' blind siding with the challenger. The engine already holds most of what the rc needs: 2.8.0's adjudication at every seat by truth, the third seat on an audit's disagreement, the pots topped up and the fee destroyed, the plant's dispute and appeal at the real rates, the state's seat past $w_R$. What is new in the engine is the orchestrator's own initial verdict under an alias, the obligation at the verdict without `market_audit_only`'s other meanings, two further counters for the dial, and the readings. What is new in the instrument is the inverted challenge at a set rate and a second posting credit. The three corrections below are each a place where the rc's words, read against the engine, would do something the rc's own rules forbid or leave undefined.

## The corrections

### A. The planted approval's initial seat must be nobody's — the summons pass included

The rc has the planted approval live the plant's life to submission (§2.2) and receive its F "at a delay from the submission drawn from the trailing real initial verdicts'". Read against the engine: a submitted root with no open initial review is a seat waiting. The driver's seating step offers it to a free member (`assign_initial`), and the engine's summons pass takes it past $w_R$ — since 2.8.0 (C), on a plant, by the orchestrator's own verdict (`_state_verdict_on_plant(c, "initial", tick)`: the truth with probability $1 − ε_T$, N on a bad plant). On run 24's world the real initial verdict's delay from submission is 1 tick for 85–88% of roots, 2 for 12–14%, 3 for ≤ 0.6% (`results/redteam/stability/2.9.0-rc1-review/`: the seating gap 0/1/2 with the review itself always one tick). With $w_R$ = 2 seating steps, a planted approval drawn at delay 2 or 3 is a seat that has waited $w_R$ at the pass before its verdict — and whenever no free eligible reviewer is on hand (the pass's condition) the state's N pre-empts the planted F; whenever one is, the driver's seating would give the seat to a member unless the instrument withholds it.

**The correction.** A planted approval is marked at its posting (the engine knows it: `post_plant(truth, approval=True)`), and from its submission to the orchestrator's verdict **its initial seat is nobody's**: `assign_initial` refuses it, the summons pass skips it (the 1.2.0 skip restored for this one seat), the reserve's waiting count may count it as a real waiting case does (its wait is drawn from theirs — a reading, below). The verdict is the orchestrator's operation (`plant_verdict(cid)`), taken by the instrument in the review pass at the drawn position when the drawn delay has elapsed. The rc's §2.2 should say the seat is nobody's in so many words, as 2.8.0 (C) said the seat past $w_R$ was.

### B. The obligation at the verdict is not `market_audit_only`

The rc records the planted approval's audit obligation "at odds 1, as market_audit_only records a troop's own delivery (I25)". In the engine `market_audit_only` means three things at once: the odds are 1 (`_live_audit_odds`), the obligation is recorded at the verdict (`_initial_verdict`), and **only a member audits it — the summons pass never summons for it and counts the seat as held** (`_summon_troops`: `if c.market_audit_only: held_now += 1`). The third contradicts the rc's own rule that the seat is filled "by a free eligible member, or the orchestrator's own verdict past $w_R$ (2.8.0 (C))". I25's predicate for `market_audit_only` ("the state's own work vested without the market's audit"; "a troop audited the state's own work") is about the state's deliveries and does not fit either.

**The correction.** The planted approval carries its own mark (`planted_approval` on the case), with: `audit_odds_at_verdict` = 1 and `_live_audit_odds` = 1; the obligation recorded at the verdict (`audit_obligation`, `audit_rolled_at`, `open_items`) as the troop path does; the audit seat filled at a real audit seat's step — a member, or past $w_R$ the state's verdict through the plant branch the pass already has. I43 states the obligation at the verdict; I25 is left to the state's deliveries. The rc's §2.2 and §7 should name the mark and drop the "as market_audit_only" clause, keeping "as I25 records a troop's own delivery" for the obligation alone.

### C. The planted verdict's review is a system review, and I20 must know it

The rc records the verdict "as a review — a review id, the verdict F, a bond, a time — under a plant reviewer alias", "not adjudicated". In the engine a review is created by `_new_review`, which checks the reviewer's eligibility as an actor (`_eligible`: on duty, no open review, the bond fundable, duty-eligible — `self._actor(reviewer)` on a name that is no actor raises), locks the bond on the reviewer's wallet and sets `Actor.open_review`. A `plant_rev:` alias is no actor. The troop's seat already shows the other road (`_summon_review`): the `Review` built directly, the bond locked on the troop id through `Ledger.aliases`, no actor touched, and every reviewer lookup in the engine guarded by `is_troop` — seventeen guards (settlement: no emission, no uplift, the fixed fee paid; `_overturn_review`; `_check_record`; the rotation; the reveal). And I20's per-seat check (`_I20_every_seat`) fails any done F or N on a plant "without adjudication" and any troop on a plant.

**The correction.** The planted verdict's review is built on the troop seat's road under a third system identity — the alias an entry in `Ledger.aliases` to a plant wallet (`PLANT_ACT`, or a third plant wallet `PLANT_REV` if the readings want its holdings apart), the bond minted to it (`mint_plant`) and locked, `cost` 0 — and the seventeen guards become one predicate (`is_system_reviewer`: a troop or a plant reviewer alias) where the alias must behave as a troop does (no actor, no record, no emission, no uplift, no rotation), with two differences the rc states: **no fixed fee** (`fee_owed` 0; a troop reviewer is paid its fee) and **no adjudication** (the review's `plant_outcome` a fourth value, `planted`, exempt from I20's "without adjudication" and from the adjudication count; I20's "a troop sat on a plant" reads `is_troop`, not the alias). At the case's closure the review is settled as stood-without-fee, its bond released to the alias's wallet and destroyed with the pots' remainder (`retire_plant`), never burned as a forfeit (`burn_bond` is a real reviewer's penalty and a different sink in the bank's record). The case's `plant_outcome` names the planted verdict (`planted`, beside `state`), so `_adjudicate_plant` never runs on it: the dial's `period["plants"]`, `bad`, `bad_approved` and the plant credit are untouched, which is the rc's "the dial's clock does not count it". The rc's §7 should say the review is a system review on the troop seat's road, not a member's review under another name.

## Corrections of fact

1. **"Per real pending approval" needs a real count the engine does not keep.** `period["approvals"]` counts every root's F — the plants' too (2.8.0 §5: the reserve's audit demand counts a plant's pending F). $π^A$'s base is the period's **real** initial Fs; the engine should carry a `planted_approval_credit` incremented by $π^A_t$ at each real root's initial F in `_initial_verdict` (as `plant_credit` is incremented by $π_t$ at each real initial assignment), with `planted_approvals_due()` beside `plants_due()`. The rc's §2.7 figure holds: real approvals ~1,900–2,100 a run on run 24's world (seeds 21–23), so ~38–42 planted approvals a run at the floor and ~760–850 at the cap.
2. **"Per real appeal / per real dispute of the period" is known at the period's end.** The instrument files during the period; the rates must read the trailing closed period's real count (as the dial's readings do), with the floor of two applying when that count is zero or the product is under two. The rc's §2.3 should say "of the trailing period".
3. **The real challenges' delay windows can be empty.** The rc draws the planted lie's timing "from all real challenges of the kind"; with disputes off there are no real disputes, and the floor of two then files a lying dispute with no window to draw from. 2.8.0's precedent is the revision window ("while empty, the plant is spent"); here the lie must still be filed, so the rc should name the fallback — the truthful plant disputes' own delays, or a stated default (0 ticks, the real requesters' delay in every standing cell).
4. **The grounds are one string.** In every standing cell the real appeals carry "the work meets the request" and the real disputes "the result does not meet the request" — truthful and lying alike (`--appeal-lie`, `--dispute-lie` file the same text). The panel's view carries the grounds (`objection_text`), so the planted lie must carry the population's string, which "drawn as the real challenges' are" gives; the rc's §2.3 can say so plainly and the stage check need not test it.

## Settled by the reviewer's ruling of October 27 (carried into the rc.2 text under §2.6)

**`audit_odds_at_verdict` joins the masked fields.** For a planted approval the public surface shows the field at a value drawn from the trailing real approvals' odds until the reveal and the true 1 after; the roll reads the engine's 1 by the orchestrator's road (`audit_odds(cid)`), not the public field; a troop's own-delivery approval at 1 is a real case and stays. Implementation: the draw is made once at the verdict and stored (`audit_odds_public`), so repeated reads agree; the engine keeps a window of the real approvals' recorded odds (the `_recent` windows carry ceilings, taus and deliveries today; one more column); `_pub_case` masks the field by `_masked`'s rule with the stored draw as the value before the reveal. Nothing of it is built before rc.2.

## Readings

1. **The alias's Review against the engine's Review** (the reviewer's first point): as correction C — a system review on the troop's road, a bond from `mint_plant` locked on the alias, no reviewer wallet, no fee, no adjudication, destroyed with the pots at closure. The public `review(rid)` shows id, case, `plant_rev:` reviewer (the alias boundary), role, `t_assign`, `t_done`, bond, verdict F — a real review's shape. For the honest shape of its times, draw the seating gap and keep the one-tick duration (`t_assign` = `t_submit` + gap, `t_done` = `t_assign` + 1) rather than one delay with `t_assign` = `t_done`; both windows the instrument keeps.
2. **The planted approval in the period's approvals and the audit demand at odds 1** (the second point): `_initial_verdict`'s F branch does this for every root (`approvals += 1`, `audit_demand += audit_odds`), and `_state_verdict_on_plant` repeats it for the state's seat; the planted verdict's path does the same with odds 1. The member seats (`_rotate_membership`, sized on the applied odds plus the challenge rate) and the reserve target follow: at the floor +2.5% audit seats (38 on ~1,500 audits held a run), at the cap +50%. The seat demand at the cap is the cost of truth's main term and is read in §8.6.
3. **The dial's clock skipping the planted verdict** (the third point): by correction C the planted verdict never reaches `_adjudicate_plant`, which alone moves `period["plants"]`, `bad`, `bad_approved` (F̂) and the plant record; `plant_credit` moves only at a real root's `assign_initial`. The period's length is `period_ticks`, not a count; "the clock" is the rate per real initial review and F̂ over the initial adjudications — both untouched. $m^A$ moves only $π^A$ (the rc's §2.4), not the odds, $X_t$ or ω — stated, and right: the audit stamper's price is its record, not its odds.
4. **The planted lie's grounds draw** (the fourth point): as fact 4 — one string per kind in every standing cell; the view shows it; the instrument draws from the real texts, which are that string.
5. **The floor of two a period** (the fifth point): feasible on run 24's world — ~22 bad plants a period of which ~15 rejected by careful first reviewers (candidates for a lying appeal), ~7 good plants of which ~6 approved (candidates for a lying dispute). Two consequences to read: (i) in the disputes-off cells the dispute panel becomes a seat **only plants reach** (the two lying disputes a period against zero real ones) — a panelist that learns "dispute question ⇒ a plant ⇒ care" is right by truth, and §57's base-rate reading becomes 1.0 at that seat in those cells; (ii) a lying appeal spends a bad plant's N — the plant actor's "accept" today — so the plant's truthful path (final N at the response deadline) is replaced by a panel for two of them a period; the cost is two panels.
6. **Whether the planted approval's dispute path under disputes on makes (A) idle there** (the sixth point): yes, as the reviewer expects, and the engine closes it cleanly. The plant requester disputes a bad plant's F at the real rate; with `--dispute-policy truthful` the real requesters dispute 75–101 real roots a run and win 96%, so nearly every planted approval would be disputed before its audit seat is filled. The engine today: `dispute()` admits a dispute while the obligation stands (it merges an *open* audit into the panel; a standing obligation it leaves standing, `open_items` 2), the panel resolves by truth, the case goes final N and closes with the obligation still True — observed by hand on a bad plant (`results/redteam/stability/2.9.0-rc1-review/`), `check_all` clean, since I25 reads obligations only on vested cases and a planted approval is always bad. Two readings beside: a real case in the same position (rolled, then disputed and the dispute *lost*) would stay PENDING with the obligation undischarged and the driver no longer wanting the seat — it does not happen in the standing cells (0 of 3–4 lost disputes a run, seeds 21–23, both rules), because the real requesters dispute in the pass before the roll; a sentence in the rc or an erratum could let the dispute's panel discharge a standing obligation (`_settle_plant_challenge` / `_settle_challenge`: `audit_obligation = False, open_items −= 1`), a conformance detail I would land with the revision. And (A)'s lever under disputes on is weak by construction: the audit seat meets a planted approval only through the ~4% the requesters leave undisputed, so H1 is a disputes-off hypothesis and the disputes-on chain reads the panels (B) — the rc's §8.2 order already has it so.
7. **The goldens moving once** (the seventh point): the golden scenario (`tests/test_perf_equivalence.py`: `plants_due` → `post_plant`, `revise_plant`) is the instrument's; it moves only if it drives the new operations. I would extend it to post planted approvals and file planted lies (as 2.8.0 extended it to `revise_plant`), so the goldens move once at the implementing commit and pin the new path; the flags alone, with no instrument posting, move nothing.
8. **`EXPECTED_FAIL` 32** (the eighth point): the reviewer tests post no plants and I43–I44 are vacuous on their worlds; I20's amendment touches plants only. 32 stands. One strict check to keep: an expected failure that passes still fails the run.
9. **`test_v29`'s regressions as listed in §7** (the ninth point): the worked example (§6) as the spine; I43, I44, I42's extension; the dial per seat's bounds and the undefined reading leaving a multiplier; the planted approval's author absent from the auditor's ReviewView and its 45 fields (the view's shape equal to a real pending F's); audited exactly once at odds 1, the obligation recorded at the verdict, discharged by the member's audit or the state's verdict past $w_R$ (the summons pass's plant branch, correction B); the state never takes the planted approval's initial seat (correction A); the planted lie's first reviewer not adjudicated twice (2.8.0's "each reviewer once" holds the panel's adjudication apart from the initial's); the `log` reader still reading nothing and the masked fields still masked on a planted approval (the public `audit_odds_at_verdict` the drawn value before the reveal, 1 after); the alias's review paid no fee, no emission, no uplift and its bond destroyed, not burned; a lying appeal on a bad plant and a lying dispute on a good plant adjudicated by truth with the fee destroyed; the planted approval counted in the period's approvals and the audit demand at 1 and not in the dial's plants, bad or bad-approved.
10. **The readings of §5.** All constructible from what the engine records: planted approvals posted / audited / confirmed / rejected / third seats / disputed first from the case's mark and its `plant_adjudications`; the three F̂ and multipliers in the period record beside the dial's; planted lies filed and sided with by kind from the challenge's kind and the plant's truth (a lie is a plant challenge against its truth — no new flag); the bad plants a member meets per seat kind per period from `plant_adjudications` tallied by reviewer and role (the count the record sees — the reading that says whether the audit seat's count has reached the first review's order); panel-ineligible members per period with the cause from `_check_record`'s crossings by the seat that failed; the cost of truth split by the mint's case kind. One more reading I would add: the share of planted approvals whose initial seat the state would have taken under 2.8.0's pass (drawn delay ≥ $w_R$ with no free reviewer at the pass) — the size of correction A.

## For the record

- **Nothing a participant may do changes** (§4 as written): a member's seats, bids, challenges and departures are as 2.8.0's; what changes is where the orchestrator's known truth appears and at what rate. §19: a design revision, minor.
- **The alias names stay the boundary.** `plant_rev:` joins `plant_req:` and `plant_act:`; on the view a planted approval is distinguishable by its reviewer's name and by nothing else once `audit_odds_at_verdict` is masked (the reviewer's ruling of October 27); on the convention's surface by nothing.
- **The honest shape, read against the engine's own verdicts.** A real initial verdict takes one tick after seating; the state's seat takes none. A planted verdict drawn from the real delays is a real case's shape; a planted verdict the state's shape would be the half-capture §1 names. Correction A keeps the state off the seat.
- **The one-slot figure.** The audit-only stamper (§8.7) is the arithmetic's one-slot reading under 2.8.0 (+2 ± 70 against the careful, §59); under 2.9.0 it is the first hand check of H1 — off panels in proportion to its stamps once the count at the audit seat is the dial's.
