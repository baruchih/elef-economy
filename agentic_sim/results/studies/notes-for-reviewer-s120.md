### 120b. AC2.1 — the road as built under `trusted_ruling`, a line each

- **(a) Two fresh panelists; members where free, the state's troops otherwise — confirmed, with the wait stated.** An appeal opens a challenge record with no review on it (an appeal is of an N; no audit sits on a rejection). The driver seats the panel one seat at a tick from the members on duty that are free, panel-eligible (the earned seats held, θ_panel) and not excluded from the case — the initial reviewer, the actor and the requester are excluded (`_eligible`, `c.excluded`). A seat unfilled for w_R = 2 seating steps with no free panel-eligible member is taken by a troop in the tick's summons pass (`_summon_review(c, "appeal")`). So a panel is two members, one of each, or two troops, by what was free at each seat's turn; in this cell the state holds 42 in 100 of the appeal panel's seats (0.033 a root against the members' 0.045).
- **(b) Both confirm → the verdict stands, the case closed, no further round — confirmed.** `_maybe_settle_challenge` under arm 1 on a member's delivery: both panel verdicts equal to the standing verdict → `_settle_challenge(outcome = the verdict, by "appeal reviewers")` → final N (`_finalize_N`, "appeal upheld nonfulfillment"). No seat is overturned, no bond moves; the appellant's court fee, paid to Ω at the filing, is not returned. The one challenge round per case (§8) is spent: nothing further can be filed.
- **(c) Otherwise the deciding verdict is taken and is the outcome — confirmed.** Any other pair (one or both panelists differing from the standing verdict) escalates: `ch["escalated"]`, `_open_deciding(c, "panel")` — two seats of the orchestrator's own inspectors on the troop seat's road without the w_R wait, answered the next tick at ε_T, a third on a split, the majority the outcome (`_deciding_result`), settled `by "deciding verdict"`. An F overturns the initial reviewer (bond burned, the court fee to the appellant) and returns the case to PENDING for its window; an N is final. The panel never overturns on its own word; `rule` is refused on a member's delivery under arm 1 (the rulings 0.09 a run here are on the orchestrator's own deliveries, A1.3).
- **(d) A panelist is overturned only against the deciding verdict or a plant's truth — confirmed.** At a both-confirm settlement nothing is overturned. At a deciding settlement each member panelist whose verdict differs from the deciding outcome is overturned (`_overturn_review`: the bond burned, the record −1, the odds doubled, the streak reset), the one that agrees with it stands though it differed from the standing verdict. So a panelist that escalated rightly is not punished, and a panelist that confirmed a rejection the deciding verdict overturns is. A plant's seats are adjudicated by the truth at the verdict (2.8.0, `_adjudicate_plant_seat`). The false penalties at the appeal seat — a member panelist overturned against a deciding verdict the truth disagrees with — are 0.96 a run in this cell.
- **(e) The same whoever sits — confirmed, with one difference stated.** The panel's rule reads the two verdicts and nothing of who gave them; a troop's seat and a member's are seated, answered and settled on one road, and a troop on an appeal panel answers as every troop's seat does (the truth with probability 1 − ε_T). The difference: at a deciding settlement a troop panelist whose verdict differs from the deciding outcome is never overturned (A1 §3, the orchestrator's own seats; `is_system_reviewer` skipped in `_settle_challenge`) where a member's is. A ⊥ appeal has its own road (the objection), never taken: no population of record has answered P or ⊥.
