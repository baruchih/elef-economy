## Two readings for your decision before the data

Both are the implementer's, both are marked in the text (§12.1, Appendix H), and the alternative to each is one edit.

1. **The members' cap never falls below the reserve's floor V_max + 1.** Your cap ⌈E_t/2⌉ is 3 at six actors — below the X₀ = 4 of Appendix A's initialization and §20's worked example (C, D, E, F on duty), so the baseline's own worlds, and every reviewer test built on them, would violate the revision at tick 0 (the first suite run failed across the board on "C is not on verification duty"). The engine lets the cap yield to the reserve floor as your panel floor yields to the cap. The difference from the literal text is confined to memberships of four to seven, where the literal cap would leave two or three members free; at 20 actors and 60/40 (eight members, cap four) the two readings agree.
2. **An outgoing probationer whose turn ends with no free probationer stays.** Replacing it with a member would seat a member in an initial seat that the next tick's "probationers first" hands back — churn, not a rule.
