## A conformance point first, and the correction it forces

§7.5 of the baseline reads: *"Volunteers first; otherwise round-robin among eligible duty actors; otherwise wait."* That is a Rule.

1. **Our own driver.** Every sweep of the 1.1.0 study (October 16–17) assigned initial, audit and appeal reviewers by a seeded random choice among the eligible duty actors, and declared that policy in every manifest under §18.2. Declared, so honest; but not the rule's policy. The driver now defaults to round-robin among the eligible duty actors (the next eligible actor in id order after the one assigned last) and keeps the seeded choice behind `--assignment seeded`, which reproduces the earlier heads. The four headline cells re-run under round-robin (`rr-check/`):

   Every cell moved by less than its standard error and no direction changed. I propose the five earlier sweeps stand as declared, with this note recorded in the hand-off (done).

**The test** (your item 5). The random scenario in `tests/test_perf_equivalence.py` already exercises the closure cross-pot path under the per-tick audit: Ω's advances are repaid from the fee pot at closure 5 and 3 times in the two seeds, with 22 and 17 nested draws on the root's pots. ## What this leaves for the revision 2 proposal

Three measured inputs now decide the careful–careless contest under 1.1.0: whether the party who knows the truth challenges (break-even dispute rate ≈ 0.07 at 50/50), how often the careful reviewer errs (break-even ε ≈ 0.08 at dispute rate 0.25), and who sits on the panel (a stamper-majority reserve is not recovered by any dispute rate). The first two are the product's inputs; the third is the rule's. Questions for the proposal, in the order I would put them:

1. The panel's composition — §7.5's round-robin against a panel chosen by ρ, or with a reliability floor. The one question the runs cannot answer without a rule change.
2. Whether an appealable error and an auditable error should carry the same bond, given exposure rates that differ by a factor of thirty (a ≈ 0.98 against an effective catch rate ≈ 0.027).
3. The reserve's size against the challenge load — a default (X), with the capacity cost measured above.
4. The cost of a dispute against an audit — defaults D and X first, as you proposed.
