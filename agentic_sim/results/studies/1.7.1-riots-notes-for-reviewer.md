## For the decision

The riots the brief named were carried by the auto-scale alone and retracted in two periods; the clock and Ω's order ran where the capital bound — the flood's ramp, and a spike at two and a half times the quiet capacity — and worked as written, with one consequence to weigh: past the capital edge the liability is a stock paid down at the market's income, about 6 a tick at 60/40, so a hard spike leaves tens of periods of debt during which Ω holds only the plant reserve and nothing the spike's requesters were told. Whether that is "something breaks" is your call; if it is, it is the cannot-serve protocol's case exactly (a summons refused for funds, a request past its window with nobody coming), and the protocol's trigger has a measured edge to be defined against. If it is not, the protocol stays a product question and nothing in these 460 runs asks for a rule.
