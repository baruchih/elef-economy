## Implementation readings, for the record

- **"Reviews waiting" is counted by kind, and I9's amendment is per kind under the cap.** An initial seat can be filled by a probationer or a member; a panel seat (a rolled audit without an open audit review, a challenge short of a seat) by a determined passing member only, under the members' cap. So the queue the reserve is sized on has two parts — root submissions without an open initial review; obligations and challenge seats without one — and I9's clause "on-duty seats are at least the reviews waiting whenever eligible free actors exist to seat" is checked as: initial seats at least the initial queue while free eligible probationers or members exist; member seats at least the panel queue while free determined members exist *under the cap*; the rest is the eligibility shortfall. Without the cap in the predicate, every tick in which audits wait with the four member seats full and only probationers free would fail an invariant the rules themselves make true.
- **Ω's order within the tick.** Liabilities older than a period are paid at the start of step 5, before the summons pass; the pass advances for review seats then deliveries from the balance above the plant reserve; younger liabilities are paid after the pass. The plant reserve's "ticks remaining" is `period_ticks` less the ticks elapsed — the period may end sooner on `period_plants`, which makes the reserve conservative, never short.
- **`--rules 1.5.0` as the control** turns all five flags off: $A_T$ binds at 100, the 1.5.0 one-period reset stands (`reserve_periods=1`), the panel rule is off, $X_t$ is the period's sizing, no clock. `reserve_periods=2` is the 1.4.0 engine's window over 1.5.0's $D_s$, as §2.5 says.
