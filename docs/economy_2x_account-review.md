**Verdict. Accurate as an account, with four corrections of fact and a handful of wording notes.** Nothing in it warrants a rule, a test or an errata entry. The corrections are the author's to apply; the text is kept here as uploaded.

## Corrections of fact

1. **§0 and §6.1 — "§18.1 q7 and q10 from v1.0".** v1.0's §18.1 has eight questions; q7 is *Monetary behavior* — opened by 2.0.0 and read in runs 12–14 (issuance, the deficit, capacity, hoards), so not untouched — and q10, added by 1.2.0, is *Constructibility*: what fraction of real work in a target domain admits plants. Neither is "the loop's long-run reliability dynamics" or "the human-override path". What the record leaves open: q10 (a domain question no run can answer), q9's alarm under adaptation (read under prices in run 12's adaptive and drift cells), and the human override, which is §15.2's path and a design assumption, not a §18.1 question. Suggested: "q10 (constructibility) and the human-override assumption".
2. **§8, first row — "no rule of 2.0.0's has been withdrawn".** Appendix N records that 2.0.0's creation, retirement and the deficit were *retired as rules* by 2.1.0 and kept as the control (`--rules 2.0.0`). The approach held — a floating price, an unbacked credit, the state never broke — and the state's own printing did not survive the first minor. Suggested: "the approach has held through five one-rule revisions; the one 2.0.0 mechanism retired, Ω's creation and the deficit, was replaced by the bank's loans in the first of them".
3. **§0 and §4.3 — "the lagging auditor zero at every cell of every run".** Zero in every quiet cell from run 12 on (the panel rule holds under prices), but run 12's drift cells read the lagging kind at 10.8 / 3.9 of 18.6 / 7.85 upheld false approvals — the plant record's lag under a capture wave, as 1.6.0 read it — and run 17's cycler sits on initial seats with probation false approvals of 8.5 a run against 0.3. Suggested: "zero in every quiet cell; the drift wave and the cycler are the readings that are not, and are read as the record's lag".
4. **§4.5 — "the bank's loans a few hundred, the debt a few".** Since 2.2.0 the riots' loans peak in the tens and the debt in single digits: run 15's crowd and flood 62 / 5, run 18's crowd 45.9 / 6.6. "A few hundred" is run 14's 1,700 before the rebate stopped draining the state's working capital — which §4.2 states correctly as 1,700 → 62. Suggested: "the bank's loans peak in the tens, the debt in single digits".

## Wording notes — the record says it slightly differently

- **§2 Money, "burns (forfeited bonds and guarantees …)".** A forfeited guarantee goes to the requester or the parent — a transfer (`forfeit_to`), not a burn. The ledger's burns are the forfeited reviewer bond's remainder (`burn_bond`), effort and the troops' costs (`purchase`), the bank's `repayment` and `retire_plant`, and `departure`. Suggested: "forfeited reviewer bonds".
- **§2 Money, "the bank issues each customer 10 a tick".** The drivers' declaration (`--issuance` 10, every §8 cell); the engine's default is 0 so the reviewer tests see none. Suggested: "issues each customer what the run declares — 10 a tick in every cell run".
- **§2 the busy line, "a credit a tick late, capped at the price".** $r_s$ defaults to $\omega_0 B$ (1 at $\bar B = 20$; the 1.7.0 $\omega_0$ sweep pinned it at 1), indexed at the case's first lock since 2.2.0's conformance correction.
- **§1, "50–80% on time".** Reads as a rate. Run 10's reading is on time 1.00 from the second period on at the 50–80% splits, with liability 0 and refusals 0.
- **§4.4 Identity.** The cycler costs the loop a quarter of the honest admissions (11.9 against 15.3) and a third more time to membership (441 against 317 ticks), not "a third of the honest cohort's admission time"; its own bill is 2,000 of plant mint and 8.5 probation false approvals a run.
- **§4.1 run 12.** The stampers' real wealth fell 13%; "a tenth" rounds down.
- **§7, "twenty-eight reviews" and "three conformance corrections".** Twenty-six implementer's reviews are on disk (dr2–dr13, two or three each) and dr1's two are recorded in HANDOFF's history table; the ten external reviews R01–R10 are in `reviews/`. The three engine corrections with regression tests are 1.7.1 (the award summons per request), 2.2.0 ($r_s$ indexed at the first lock) and 2.4.0 (the award pass gathers at $p_T$); the 1.4.0 roll held as an obligation was a correction of the drivers. Right as written if those are the counts meant.
- **§8, the criteria "set when the question was first asked (October 19, after run 13)".** Not in this repository's record — HANDOFF carries no learned-red-team criteria. Stated by the author; not verified here.
