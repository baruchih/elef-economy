# Run 24 — the appeals baseline, the calibration cell's cost, the rolling window (the rulings after run 23)

Notes for the design reviewer. Three evaluations under fixed rules, in the order ruled after run 23 (Appendix S): the population's appeals
baseline (ruling 4), the calibration cell's care cost (ruling 5), the plant's draws on a rolling window and Q2's probe re-read with errors
(ruling 6). Code at `09622b85`; everything under `results/studies/2.6.0-appeals/`, `results/redteam/calibration/`, `results/redteam/Q2/`.

## 1. What went in

- **Truthful appeals the population's default** (`drivers.stamping --appeal-policy truthful`; `none` reproduces runs 1–23). An actor whose
  work was truly fulfilled appeals an N, paying the court fee D when its unlocked credit covers it; a stamper's false approval is not an
  appeal's matter (the requester's dispute policy stays `none`). Every learned question from here trains and evaluates in this population.
- **The care-cost knob** (`--care-cost`, a multiple of the drawn {2, 3} credits; 1.0 the population's). Raised in the calibration cell only.
- **The rolling window** (a conformance item, no flag): the plant's two draws from the trailing real roots — the ceiling at posting, the
  time to submission at the award — read the last k real roots' terms, k the count the last `trailing_periods` closed periods hold, the
  current period's roots included; before the first closed period the defaults as before. The closed periods' own box, whose contents
  changed only at a boundary, carried the period's rhythm into every plant's terms; the window does not. The reviewer named the ceiling; I
  applied the same reading to the submission draw, which §7.6 words the same way ("the trailing real deliveries'") — declared, for the
  reviewer to confirm or undo. `test_v26` 17; both goldens re-pinned once at the correcting commit, as at the Appendix S ruling after run 21.
- **Appendix S** records run 23's acceptance and the seven rulings.

## 2. The appeals baseline (ruling 4) — what moves when the population appeals truthfully

Run 16's world under 2.6.0 (`--entry-pool 20 --stampers 0.6`), appeals on against off, seeds 1–20 (`results/studies/2.6.0-appeals/`; 40 runs,
every per-tick check and final audit passed). The population's workers appeal an N on work truly fulfilled when they can fund the court fee;
the requesters' disputes stay off. Means over the seeds, ± the standard error:

| reading (per run of 1000 ticks) | appeals off (runs 1–23's population) | appeals on (the default from run 24) |
|---|---|---|
| careful's wealth (nominal / real) | 1378 ± 33 / 1254 ± 31 | 1402 ± 29 / 1340 ± 24 |
| vested / final N | 1907 ± 13 / 599 ± 8 | 1950 ± 17 / 510 ± 6 |
| false rejections stood (N on fulfilled work, final) | 105.0 ± 2.4 | 22.9 ± 0.8 |
| false rejections made / appealed / overturned | 87.1 / 0 / 0 | 84.9 / 84.3 / 81.2 (96 %) |
| appeals filed / unfunded / won / lost | 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 | 84.3 / 0.6 / 81.2 / 2.0 |
| careful's reviews overturned / bonds forfeited | 4.5 ± 0.2 / 23.1 ± 0.9 | 9.0 ± 0.2 / 44.4 ± 1.4 |
| false approvals | 13.0 ± 1.0 | 11.0 ± 0.9 |
| suspensions / admissions | 15.6 / 15.3 | 14.8 / 15.4 |
| Ω's fee income / Ω net | 2628 ± 23 / 4659 ± 71 | 2473 ± 15 / 4601 ± 70 |
| P_t at the end | 22.2 ± 0.7 | 21.0 ± 0.5 |

**What moves.** Almost every false rejection is appealed and almost every appeal wins (81 of 84): final N falls by 89 a run, vesting rises by 43,
and the stood false rejections fall from 105 to 23 — what remains is the ones no appeal reaches (the few unfunded, the two a run lost, and the
rejections of nested or late work the policy does not cover). The careful reviewer pays for its own 5 % of wrong rejections now: its overturned
reviews double and its forfeited bonds double (23 → 44 nominal), yet it ends richer in real terms (+86 ± 39), because the market vests more and
the price level is a point lower. Court fees reach Ω only from the two lost appeals a run — the reading the reviewer asked for: they were zero
through run 23 and are now about 2 D a run; Ω's fee income falls by 155, the final Ns it no longer collects on. Nothing else moves outside noise:
false approvals, suspensions, admissions, the dial. An evaluation under fixed rules; from here every cell runs in this population unless it says
otherwise, and `--appeal-policy none` reproduces runs 1–23.

## 4. The rolling window and the probe re-read (ruling 6) — the ceiling was not the carrier

**The window** went in as ruled (§1) and the probe was re-read on it: 80 offline episodes (careful, stamper, ε, random × seeds 1–20) in run 16's
world with appeals on (the population from here) and, as the control, with appeals off (the population Q2 was closed in); the world model on
each; the logistic probes on the latent, the raw observation, the context alone and the case's fields alone, trained on seeds 1–10 and scored on
11–20, among the initial reviews; the reviewer's criterion read as the **paired gap AUC(raw) − AUC(context)** with its seed-level bootstrap
error (the same resample scores both probes; `redteam.probe`, `auc_gap_raw_minus_context`). One correction to run 22's figures first: run 22's
SE probe (`probe-2.6.0-se-initial.json`, 0.679 against 0.629) read 280 episodes — the ten online rounds write their episodes beside the offline
ones — so every re-read here, run 22's construction included, is on the 80 offline episodes alone (`--policies careful,stamper,eps,random`):

| construction (initial reviews, test seeds 11–20) | raw | context | **gap raw − context** | case's fields alone | field adding most (paired) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| run 22's (the closed period's box), appeals off | 0.666 ± 0.008 | 0.614 ± 0.007 | **+0.052 ± 0.005** | 0.600 ± 0.016 | `rv_gap` +0.016 ± 0.006, `seen_ticks_since` +0.012 ± 0.008 |
| the window, appeals off | 0.654 ± 0.020 | 0.595 ± 0.017 | **+0.059 ± 0.012** | 0.581 ± 0.016 | `rv_gap` +0.051 ± 0.013 |
| the window, appeals on | 0.672 ± 0.016 | 0.629 ± 0.011 | **+0.043 ± 0.013** | 0.560 ± 0.018 | `rv_gap` +0.032 ± 0.007 |

(± the seed-level SE throughout.) **The window closed nothing**: the gap is where it was, three to five seed-level errors from zero, and the
per-field ablation (`--ablate-case`: each case field alone, added to the context, removed from the raw) names one carrier — `rv_gap`, the
submission-to-assignment gap, which adds +0.03 to +0.05 by itself; every other case field adds nothing beyond ±0.005; the ceiling's fields
(`rv_cap_rel`, `rv_bond_rel`, `seen_beta_rel`) add zero to the third decimal in every construction. The window is still the right reading of
"trailing" and stays; it was not the leak.

**The carrier, read from the engine.** Among initial reviews plants are seated the tick they submit 94 % of the time, real work 86 %. Five
careful runs under the instrument's eye (the driver's cases, not the view): a plant waited **1.13 ticks in the market** before its award, a
real root 0.41; their lags from award to submission were the same (1.08 and 1.00); in a tick with several submissions the plant was the
**oldest** of them (its rank among the tick's submitters by posting 0.19, real work 0.58) and the working set's oldest-first seating gave it
the free seat: seated later 4.4 % against 16.2 %. The extra tick in the market is the instrument's: the plants due were posted **after the
tick's award pass** — 1.2.0's place for a plant created submitted, which only needed a seat — so under 2.6.0 a plant could never be awarded
the tick it was posted, as a real root posted by a requester before the pass can be, and arrived at its seat a tick older than everything
beside it. Not the ceiling, not the rhythm: the step order of the orchestrator's instrument, the same class of effect as the submission
correction after run 21, one step upstream.

**The correction** (`drivers.stamping`, `plant_step`; code `64b82232`): under 2.6.0 the plants due are posted in the requesters' posting
step, before the awards, each at a **drawn position** among the tick's postings; under 2.5.0 and before they are created submitted where they
were. The position has to be drawn: posted last among the tick's postings (the first version of the move) the plant was the *youngest* at
submission and seated last — 30.6 % later against 10.1 % — the same tell with the sign reversed. Drawn, the rank is 0.51 against 0.50, seated
later 18.4 % against 14.5 %. What remains of the market wait (1.40 against 0.63 in the mean, 86 % against 77 % awarded the tick posted) is
not position: a real root posted below the market is **raised** by its requester after a wait (35 % of real roots are raised before their award)
and a plant's ceiling, drawn once from the ceilings real roots were *awarded* at, is never raised — so the plant is taken at once more often
and, when it is not, waits to w_A for the floor. That is the construction's own shape, a plant-requester that does not raise, and it is the
reviewer's matter (§5), not the instrument's.

**The verdict on the criterion: not met, at a third of the distance.** On the corrected construction the case's fields add 0.016–0.019 of AUC
beyond the context, four seed-level errors from zero in both populations (the appeals-off pass at ten seeds was the resolution, not the
construction); the gap was 0.052–0.059 before the correction. No case field adds more than +0.007 on its own; `rv_gap`, the carrier the

plant's ceiling is drawn once and never raised, a real root's is raised by its requester after a wait in 35 % of awards, so the time a root is
seen available before its award is distributed differently for the two, and a member that saw the root in the market carries that into the
review. It is in the convention (the seen set is the member's own observation) and it is the construction's, not the instrument's: the rule
says the ceiling is drawn once.


## 5. For the decision

1. **The appeals baseline** (§2) is the record ruling 4 asked for: appeals overturn 96 % of the false rejections, final N falls by 89 a run,
   careful ends richer in real terms though its forfeits double, court fees reach Ω at about 2 D a run. The population from here appeals.
2. **The calibration cell is at care-cost 4** (§3): the ideal clears careful by 5.8 SE at twenty seeds; 3 gives 2.0. The learner of run 23 is
   not re-run in it (its calibration stands per version); the next learner version is.
3. **The window stays; the leak was the instrument's.** The ceiling draw never carried the separation; the seating gap did, because the plants
   were posted after the award pass. Corrected in the driver (a drawn position among the tick's postings), declared here and in Appendix S as a
   conformance correction of the orchestrator's instrument, the same class as the submission correction after run 21. The reviewer records or
   undoes it. The lesson I draw for the record: every step the instrument takes for a plant must sit where a real root's would, and in a drawn
   position among them — the posting and the submission are now both there; the award is the market's already.
4. **The criterion is not met on the corrected construction: +0.016 ± 0.004 and +0.019 ± 0.005** at twenty test seeds, down from +0.052. The
   residual is diffuse, with the time a root was seen available the largest piece (§4), and it points at the one remaining asymmetry a member
   can see within the convention: **the plant requester never raises its ceiling**, while 35 % of real roots are raised before their award.
   Three readings for the reviewer: (a) accept 0.02 at this resolution as the construction's floor and close the item (the brief's Q2 learner
   was a no-win at 0.05); (b) a conformance reading — the plant's ceiling drawn from the roots' ceilings *as posted* and the plant requester
   raising as the population's requesters do (the drivers' convention for a requester, applied to the plant's) — I would not make this one
   without a ruling, since §7.6 says the ceiling is drawn once; (c) a design item for a later revision. I lean to (a) with (b) noted.
5. **Run 22's SE figures read 280 episodes** (the online rounds' beside the offline); the numbers in the record (0.679 / 0.629 / 0.595) stand as
   read, and the 80-episode re-read of the same construction gives 0.666 / 0.614 / 0.600 with the gap +0.052 ± 0.005 — the same reading. From
   here the probe names its episodes (`--policies`).
6. **Order from here**, as ruled: 2.7.0 on its acceptance (the arrival loan; the reviewer's draft); then Q1's learned line once more under 2.7.0
   with appeals on and re-arrival on, one line; then Q3 in the population from here, its learner calibrated first at care-cost 4.
