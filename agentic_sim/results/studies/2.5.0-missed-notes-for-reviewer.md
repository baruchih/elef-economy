## In one paragraph

**The refusal does what it says and nothing else, and it exposes a one-tick fact the sketch did not have.** Under 2.5.0 a parent that tries to
cancel at the child's deadline is refused (98.5 refusals a run), the lapse runs, every lowballer's guarantee forfeits to its parent (200 a run,
about 2 a miss), and the cell is run 18's no-cancellation cell to the decimal — the roots' failures at **675**, not between 568 and 675: the
one driver step between cancelling the tick before the deadline and waiting for the lapse is the whole of that gap. The parent's run 18 move,
read in the world's clock, was a cancellation at **deadline − 1** (`eve`), which 2.5.0 admits ("a parent that wants out acts by d − 1") and
which is identical under both rules: the roots saved at 568, the guarantee released, the honest at 1,262. So under 2.5.0 a parent facing a
silent child has two moves — cancel the tick before (save the root, release the guarantee) or wait for the lapse (take the guarantee, risk the
root) — and the honest do better waiting (1,274 against 1,262; the base 1,304). The door the revision closes — the cancellation *at* the
deadline — was reachable (2.4.0 at that tick: roots 559, the lowballers whole) and is now shut. **Honest children and everything else are
untouched to the decimal** (the honest delegation cell, run 16's three spread cells, the entry cell, the patient cell at the floor, the crowd
with the pool: 2.5.0 = 2.4.0 in every column). **The red team:** a parent posting deadlines too short to meet cannot farm — the drivers'
children meet any deadline of one tick, so there is nothing to farm, and a child that reads the deadline against its own two-tick need declines
455 bids a run and leaves the farmer to self-execute; a lowballer that submits an empty result at its deadline is rejected by 81% of parents,
loses 96% of its appeals, and still does better than lapsing — and 19% of parents approve it (the careful's 5% error and the stamping
parents), building 18.8 roots a run on empty work. Nothing in the rules moves; §4 names what the reviewer may want to read.

Nothing is farmed either way. A child that reads the deadline declines 455 bids a run, the farmer's requests die at $T_{\text{wait}}$ and it

reads it and does not bid. The rc's expectation holds in the first case for the reason it gave, and in the second for a reason it did not:
the farm fails on the child's own speed. A driver reading, not a rule's.

## 5. For the decision

1. **The refusal holds and changes one cell** — the cancellation at the deadline tick, now the lapse. Hypothesis 1's roots figure was the
   sketch's reading of a driver step; the fact is that one step is the whole gap between 568 and 675.
2. **The parent's choice under 2.5.0 is `eve` or the lapse.** Cancelling the tick before is admitted by the rule's own words and leaves the
   lowballer's miss free; waiting takes the guarantee and risks the root. The honest do better waiting by 12 real. Whether a rule should
   reach d − 1 is the reviewer's question; the text as written leaves it to the parent, and I read that as intended ("the parent's choice
   and the child's protection").
3. **The guarantee floor** named as the next candidate: 2 a miss did not stop lowballing here (the parents' rule reads the bid), and the
   lowballers left the market in both cells on their earnings, not their forfeits. A reading for §18.1 Q21.
4. **The empty result** is the lowballer's better play and the parents' error is its yield (19%). Nothing for the missed-child rule; the
   chain holds at the panel's rate as run 16 found.
5. **The farm fails on the child's speed**, a driver reading; the rc's "the child reads the deadline" holds where the child needs more time
   than the deadline gives.

## 6. What is not in this run

A driver whose parents choose between `eve` and waiting by the child's bid or record (the rational parent); a lowballer whose empty result is
tuned to the parent's accuracy; a per-seed view.
