## Corrections of fact

1. **D's parenthesis — "a real case's the same number".** rc.2 §7: `audit_odds_at_verdict` is "recorded on every case at its initial verdict (a real case's the same number) and read by the audit roll." The odds a reviewer carries move at every settlement of its *other* reviews — `_vest` halves `p_audit` every `p_halve_every` stood approvals, `_overturn_review` doubles it, and `p_eff` = min(1, $m_t$·`p_audit`) moves with the dial at the boundary — so the number at the verdict and the number at the roll are the same only when nothing of the reviewer's settled between them. Measured on run 24's world (`--entry-pool 20 --stampers 0.6`, seeds 21 and 22; the driver's roll instrumented against the odds recorded at the verdict): the roll lands in the **same world tick** as the verdict (the driver's clock runs a tick ahead; the engine's vesting step has not run between them), and of the unpinned real rolls 2,121 of 2,132 and 2,481 of 2,494 read the same number; the 11 and 13 that differ (0.5%) are all **higher** — the reviewer overturned on another case in the same driver tick by a settlement or a ruling, the live odds up to doubled. So the parenthesis is right to half a percent, and the half percent runs the way D corrects: a real case rolled at the verdict's odds is rolled a shade less often than at the live ones. **Two readings, the rc's the better:** (i) as written, every case rolls at the odds of its verdict under the flag — one number, one code path, the plant and the real case audited alike by construction, §8.3's "$p_j$" read as the odds at the approval, and the real path moved by those 11–13 rolls a run (declared; the flag off keeps 2.7.0's live read, so the control is exact and §8.3 reads the difference beside the plants' seats); (ii) the plant's roll alone at the verdict's odds, a real case's live as 2.7.0 has it — §4 literal, and a plant/real asymmetry of the same half percent on the plant's side. Recommend (i), the parenthesis corrected to "the same number but for a reviewer overturned on another case in the same tick".
2. **C's wording — "joins the pass's one list beyond the free eligible members".** The one list is the **award** pass's shape (the run-34 correction: the roots past $w_A$ gathered, the free fundable members counted once, the oldest left to the market). The **seat** pass is per seat: a required seat is tested on its own — unfilled for $w_R$ seating steps *and* no free eligible on-duty reviewer for its role (`_free_eligible_reviewer(c, role)`), case by case, oldest first, audits, then initial seats, then panel seats. A plant's seats take the same test with the skip at the head of the pass (`if c.plant is not None: continue`) lifted, and at the troop's step `_state_verdict_on_plant` in place of `_summon_review`. rc.1's review introduced the phrase; §2.1's seat row, §2.4 and §7 should say "a plant's required seat unfilled for $w_R$ steps with no free eligible reviewer for its role is taken by the orchestrator's own verdict at the troop's step".

## Readings

1. **The top-up is the shortfall (A).** Appendix A's pots already pay for two seats: $\varphi^E_{\mathrm{root}} = 10 = 2K$ and $\varphi^F_{\mathrm{root}} = 5 = f\,(w_{\mathrm{initial}} + w_{\mathrm{audit}})$ — "one review and one audit", as `params.py` says beside them. "Topped up at each seat's assignment — $K$ into the allowance and $w_r f$ into the fee pot", read literally, mints for the initial seat and the first challenge seat a second time. The rule meant is the **shortfall**: when a review is created on a plant the allowance is brought to $K$ for every review not yet reimbursed and the fee pot to the fees owed, and the bank mints the difference — on the defaults nothing until the **third** seat, which is exactly where a real root's third careful reviewer reaches Ω's advance (rc.1's A). Nothing a member observes moves either way; what moves is H2's and H5's "the bank's plant mint" and the remainder destroyed at closure. One phrase in §2.2, §2.4 and §7: "topped up to the seat's need".
2. **The court fee's source — the wallet, not the pots.** §2.1's appeal row has "$D$ from the plant's pots"; §2.2 and §7 have the challenger's wallet topped up by the bank and the fee destroyed at filing. The second is the rule: the pots are the allowance and the fee pot, a court fee is the challenger's own money on a real case, and the admissibility test reads the challenger's wallet (`_open_challenge`). The plant actor's and the plant requester's fresh names alias the two plant wallets, so the top-up is `mint_plant` of $D$ to the alias and `retire_plant` of $D$ at the filing — two bank events, the names line untouched (the plant wallets are not arrivals). One phrase in the row.
3. **A panelist's bond on a plant.** On a real case a panelist loses its bond only on a split, by the ruling (`rule` overturns the wrong side); two panelists that agree never do. Under 2.8.0 each panelist is adjudicated by truth, so a panelist that agreed with its co-panelist and was wrong fails — a bond burned after a two–nothing that no real case shows. This is 1.2.0's shape at the initial seat (a reviewer learns its own verdict's plant outcome at its adjudication, its own case's field) carried to the panel, inside the design's statement in §2.2; it is read here so that "what each reviewer observes is what it would observe on a real case" is read as the same *kinds* of outcome, not the same conditional frequencies. After answering, a panelist whose agreed verdict lost knows it sat on a plant; it prices nothing. For the record, no change to the text.
4. **The audit after a dispute** — "the audit as one panel review after a dispute" (§2.2, §7) is the engine's: `_open_challenge` appends an open audit review to the challenge's reviews as `is_audit_as_appeal`, and `_maybe_settle_challenge` settles on two done reviews. Under the plant that settlement is by truth, as §2.2 has it. Confirmed.
5. **H3's "the roll in the next tick".** The next *driver* tick; the world's clock has not moved (correction 1's measurement: every real roll at a lag of zero world ticks). The stage check's "audit's lag from the verdict" is zero for every real case and will be for every plant, since the roll is the driver's for every pending F in one step; the seat's wait after the roll is the stage that can differ. A word in H3.
