## One qualifier

**The panel floor must be bounded by availability, not by membership alone.** §2.3 and I23 say on-duty members ≥ min(V_max − 1, E_t) "whenever any member is eligible", where E_t counts actors eligible for duty and not suspended. An eligible member may be committed to a task, unable to fund the bond, or mid-review elsewhere; none of those can be seated, and 1.2.0's I9 already admits a short reserve exactly when no eligible free actor was left to seat. As written, I23 would fail on a correct engine whenever the only members are busy. Proposed: "at least min(V_max − 1, E_t) members are on duty whenever that many free, fundable members exist to seat; a reserve below the floor is admissible only when none was left, and the gap counts toward the eligibility shortfall" — the same form as I9.
