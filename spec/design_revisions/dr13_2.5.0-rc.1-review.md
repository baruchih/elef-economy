# Review of 2.5.0-rc.1 — design revision 13, "the missed child"

**Date:** October 19, 2026 · **Text reviewed:** `agentic_economics_loop_spec_2.5.0-rc.1.md` · **Against:** the 2.4.0 text in force, the engine at `9ecd266b` (drivers `46c4cebe`), run 18 and its last batch · **Reviewer:** the implementer.

**Verdict. Implementable as it stands, with one correction of fact and three readings.** The revision is one refusal: `cancel_nested` declines at or after the deadline. Everything else §2.1 states as "the lapse" the engine already does — and has since v1.0 — which is the correction below; it does not change the rule, only what the text says is new.

## The correction

**The lapse is not new; the open door is the cancellation at the deadline tick.** §2.1's first rule — "a nested request awarded to $B$ whose deadline arrives without a submission is final $\mathsf N$ at that tick, after the tick's submissions have been read; $B$'s guarantee forfeits to $A$; $A$'s rejection bond is released; the pots settle at the closure; $A$ may self-execute from that tick" — is the engine's step 2 as it stands: at every `advance(tick)`, after the drivers' submissions of that tick, every committed case with `tick ≥ deadline` is finalized $\mathsf N$ ("delivery deadline missed"), `_finalize_N` forfeits the guarantee to the requester (the parent, for a nested case) and releases the rejection bond, the node closes under 2.3.0, and `_child_milestone_reached` reads `FINAL_N` as the milestone, so the parent may submit from the next step. Run 18's lowballer cell shows it: with no cancellation, 98.5 misses were 98.5 nested final $\mathsf N$ with the guarantees forfeited. What let the child go was the driver's `early` variant *using the cancellation at the deadline tick itself* — the driver's step at tick $d$ runs before the engine's `advance(d)`, so at that moment the child is still committed and `cancel_nested`, which checks only the state, succeeds and releases what the lapse an instant later would have forfeited. The revision's whole content is therefore §2.1's **second** rule: cancellation is admissible before the earlier of the submission and the deadline, and refused at `t ≥ deadline` with no effect. One condition in `cancel_nested`, one invariant, and the driver's `early` variant becomes "do not cancel at the deadline; let the lapse run and self-execute next step". I would redraft §1.2 and §2.1 to say so — the rule that was missing is the refusal, not the lapse — and keep the hypotheses as they are, since they test the right things.

## Readings

1. **The deadline tick is the child's, not the parent's.** The submission window is inclusive (`submit` refuses only at `t > deadline`), so at tick $d$ an honest child may still submit and the parent may not cancel: the refusal at `t ≥ deadline` protects the child that delivers on its last tick from a parent pre-empting it, exactly as §2.1's honest-children consequence wants. A parent that wants out must act by $d-1$.

2. **Hypothesis 1's figures will match run 18's no-cancellation cell, not improve on `early`.** Under the rule the lowballer cell becomes run 18's `cancel off` cell with one difference: the parents' rule may self-execute at the lapse (the driver already does — `parent_waiting` reads `FINAL_N` as the child failed). Run 18's `cancel off` cell had 98.5 misses and the roots' failures at 675 because the parents waited for the lapse *and then* had no room; the `early` cell saved the roots (568) by cancelling at $d$ and self-executing the same driver tick. Under 2.5.0 the parent self-executes at $d+1$ in driver time (the lapse is in `advance(d)`), one step later than `early` — I expect the roots between 568 and 675, closer to 568 since the parent's tau (work + nested room, 8) leaves six ticks after a child's two. The hypothesis's "at or under 568" may miss by that step; the direction holds.

3. **Goldens and the expected failures.** The equivalence scenario calls `cancel_nested` at random on committed children (`_try(w.cancel_nested, rng.choice(c.children))`); any such call landing at `t ≥ deadline` is now refused, which moves the path — so the goldens are re-pinned once if it does, as the rc allows. Every test that cancels (`test_spec`, `test_v05`, `test_review4_followthrough`, `test_review_regressions`' kill-fee case) cancels well before the child's deadline on my reading of their timings; I will confirm at implementation and `EXPECTED_FAIL` stays 32 unless one does not.

**Recommendation.** Accept with §1.2 and §2.1 redrafted to name the refusal as the rule and the lapse as what the engine already does (rc.2, or a reading recorded in Appendix R); nothing else to change.
