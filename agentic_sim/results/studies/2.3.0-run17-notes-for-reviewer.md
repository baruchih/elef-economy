## In one paragraph

**A. The queue before admission is a panel-eligibility queue, not a cap or a window.** In the first three hundred ticks nineteen of every
twenty seats that wait are audit or appeal seats, and while they wait there are, per waiting tick, five members on duty and idle but not
panel-eligible, eight more off duty, free, funded and duty-eligible but not panel-eligible, and ten probationers who may sit only on initial
reviews — against 0.1–0.6 members in the decisive state (free, fit for the seat, under the cap, not seated) and none held out by the cap,
whose slack is eight seats throughout. The troops take 605 of the 710 audits and 83 of the 86 appeals that wait, every one at exactly w_R;
once the cohort is admitted and determined (window 4 on) the state's share of inspection seats falls from 46% to 0.1% and the queue is
initial reviews waiting one seating step for the rotation that sizes to them. **w_R trades inspection for delivery:** 0 / 2 / 5 give the state
18 / 16 / 14% of inspection seats and 0.7 / 1.4 / 4.9% of deliveries — at 5 the queue holds eleven members on duty idle and unfit in the first
window, the cap binds a quarter of its ticks, and the members held on duty are not free to bid. **The cap does nothing:** ⅔ / ¾ / 1 are the
same run to the decimal. **The control says what the troops bridge:** with the determined-record gate off the state's inspection seats fall
from 688 to 8, and false approvals rise from 13 to 35 — the gate is doing its job, and the troops are its price. **B.** The pool's size sets
the level (60 candidates: 46 admitted, the price 14.5, the honest at 810); a float of zero is a wall (no arrival can fund the bond, none is

no eligible on-duty reviewer for it; its *wait* is the seating steps it stood unfilled (the engine's count against w_R; a seat filled at once
is never seen); *who seated it* is read from the review that opened. A seat that waits one step and is then filled by a member was filled
at the next tick's assignment after the rotation sized the reserve to it.

Two regimes. **Before admission** (the first three windows) the queue is panels: the stampers' records are over θ_panel, the careful

seven to eight members sit free, funded and fit under the cap: this is the reserve's one-step lag (the rotation sizes to the queue at the
tick's end; the assignment is the next tick's), not a shortage. The on-duty idle unfit column is the same gate seen from the reserve: the
members the rotation seats for the initial rate are of no use to an audit.

cohort's admission dips the price and the award summons runs), 11% / 6% in the fourth, then 0.1% / 0.1–0.6%. The two shares are never
high together.

**§8.7 the red team.**

*The cycler* (`--cycler on`: a suspended stamper with no live obligation departs and re-arrives at once under a fresh identity, keeping its

*The greedy parent* (half the careful actors, as parents, reject every child's work): 3.15 such rejections a run (delegation is 4% of
awards at σ 0.8), **every one appealed and every appeal won** (3.35 of 3.35; the child's work vests, 2.6 cases), the parent paying the

*The below-cost child* (a fifth of the actors bid half their cost on nested requests and never deliver): 116 lowball awards, 116 misses a

parent, so a child that bids below cost is a parent's risk the price does not carry.

The price collapses in the third window and stays at 4.5–4.8 — a quarter of the base and below the median member's cost of 8. The
mechanism is the state's at-cost delivery read against a ceiling indexed to B̄: a ceiling at 0.7 B̄ is raised to B̄ after T ticks, the troop
delivers at B̄ (c^D_T burned, the price B̄ paid), P_t counts the troop's price, B̄ (members only) follows the few member awards that fit —
the cheapest members, cost 2–4, at margins near zero (0.05–0.07) — and the next period's ceiling is 0.7 of that. Nothing stops it: members
whose cost exceeds the ceiling do not bid, earn nothing and depart (3–4 members left of 20 plus the arrivals; 1.4 admitted), the requesters
accumulate their issuance (8,600–9,500 a wallet, nothing spent), the state serves four fifths of the market at a price that covers the
cheapest members' cost and no one else's, Ω's retained balance falls to 130–260, and every request is late by design (T ticks). The honest's
"real wealth" of 4,300–5,600 is the deflated index, not earnings. **This is the strongest result in the run and a design reading, not a
strategy's:** the state's delivery at B̄ with no reservation price of its own makes the price level a free variable to a patient demand
side — the customers can wait, the state cannot refuse, and the market's cost floor is not in the rules anywhere (c_D is the drivers').
Whether the state should deliver below some floor (its expected cost, the trailing members' cost, or a declared reservation), or whether a
raise to B̄ should count as a posting at B̄ for the clock, is the reviewer's question; nothing in this run says the rules are inconsistent.

**Faster margin decay** (`--idle-k 1`: the margin falls after one idle tick instead of three, with entry): the price falls to 8.3 (20 18 9.2

**The ceiling-aware child** (`--child-bid ceiling-aware`: a child whose cost is under the parent's ceiling bids min(ceiling, c(1+m))): every

expected child — nested price 14.1 against 10.9), the gain from trade falls to 1.5 from 3.9, brokers 1.35 against 2.75 on 1.7% of awards, the
honest at 1,336 against 1,313. Run 16's three quarters dying at T_wait were the children's ratchet; a child that reads the ceiling clears
the market and takes the surplus.

## 5. For the decision

1. **The inspection queue (A; the §12.1 question).** The cap is not binding anywhere in these worlds (slack eight seats; ⅔ / ¾ / 1
   identical) and w_R only moves the state's seats between inspection and delivery. The queue the troops clear is the determined-record
   gate's — the control without it fills the seats from the market and triples false approvals. Nothing for §12.1; a reading for §7.1: the
   gate's cost is 600–700 troop panel seats per world of twenty in its first three hundred ticks, and the entry of an honest cohort ends it.
2. **The float is a gate (B, §8.5).** F_0 = 0 admits nobody because nobody can post G^V. Appendix A's 50 is a rule by another name; the
   reviewer may want §2.1 to say that the float's purpose is the bond.
3. **The cycler and the swarm (B, §8.7).** Neither reaches membership; both cost the loop plants and probation false approvals (8.5 a run
   for the cycler). §6's arithmetic holds for the cycler's wallet and is silent on the loop's bill; a reading for §8.7's text.
4. **The below-cost child (B, §8.7).** A miss costs the child half of half its bid and the parent its guarantee and pots; the driver's
   parent waits for the deadline rather than cancel. §9.1's kill fee is the rule's answer and is not exercised here — a driver gap, named.
5. **Patient customers (C).** The state's delivery at B̄ against a ceiling indexed to B̄ lets a patient demand side take the price to the
   cheapest members' cost and the state to four fifths of the market below cost. This is the one result in the run that may warrant a §19
   decision — a reservation price for the state's delivery, or the clock reading a raise as a posting — and it is the reviewer's to make.
6. **The ratchet's constant is nominal (C).** Faster decay takes the price to 8 with the honest's real wealth unchanged. The price level is
   the members' strategy's and the requesters' hoards are its mirror; nothing for the rules.

## 6. What is not in this run

A per-seed view of the queue (the tables are means over twenty seeds); the kill fee and the parent's cancellation under §9.1; a cycler
that waits out its suspension before departing (the driver's cycler departs at once, which the engine permits once the obligations are
settled); the withdrawing requester under the market-aware ceiling (run 15 had it; `told-market/` holds only).
