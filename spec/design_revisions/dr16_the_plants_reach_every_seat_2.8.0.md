# Agentic Economics — The Task, Verification, and Dispute Loop

## Design revision 16 — specification 2.8.0-rc.2: the plants reach every seat

**Project:** ELEF / Agentic Economics  
**Version:** 2.8.0-rc.2 — a release candidate, not yet in force. rc.1 (October 26) was the sketch as first sent; rc.2 settles the implementation review [I34]: **four corrections where the text met the engine** — (A) a plant's court fee is destroyed at filing and its pots are topped up at each seat's assignment, so that no coin minted for a plant reaches Ω; (B) the engine holds no truth for real work, so a revised plant's truth is the instrument's draw, re-sealed as a second marker, and a plant answered P or ⊥ stays spent until a real revision has entered the window; (C) a plant's seat past $w_R$ is taken by the orchestrator's own verdict at the troop's step, as its delivery is at the floor; (D) a plant's audit rolls at the odds its reviewer carried at the verdict, not the odds the adjudication doubled — **six corrections of fact**, chief among them that **no case in runs 1–35 was ever revised**: the "resubmissions" the record stamped were the audit seats, which strengthens §1 and empties one hypothesis; and nine readings taken into the text. On acceptance it becomes **2.8.0**, consolidated into one standalone text, and 2.7.0 (with errata 2.6.2 and the six conformance corrections) moves to history.  
**Prepared:** October 26, 2026 (rc.1–rc.2)  
**Supersedes:** 2.7.0 where this document speaks — the plant's life after its initial verdict (1.2.0 §2.1's "a plant has no challenge round") and the seats a planted case may reach — and nowhere else.  
**Nature of this revision:** A **design revision** (minor, by §19), the third the learned red team has required, and the first found at the level of a population. 1.2.0 made the orchestrator own truth where no panel and no human could be trusted to supply it: planted cases, adjudicated by construction, tested every reviewer at its first review. 1.2.0 also stopped there — *"a plant has no challenge round: no audit is rolled on it, no appeal or dispute is admitted"* — so that four seats were left where a reviewer could be certain the case before it was real: the **audit** of a pending approval, the **appeal panel**, the **dispute panel**, and the **resubmission**. Those four are the seats the loop's second layer works through: the audit prices a stamp on a first review, and the customer's dispute is the natural challenger of a wrong approval. Run 34 put every reviewer in the market under one learning policy in self-play and let the population find its equilibrium from two starts; both reached the same place: care where a plant could come, **stamp 91–97% of the seats where one could not** — and with the audit seat stamped, the audit's price of a stamp fell to zero, the population's standing false approvals rose to eight to ten times the honest population's on every seed, and the audit layer's overturn rate fell *below* the hand's with ten times the false approvals before it. Run 35 added the customer: with every requester disputing truthfully, the population's requesters won 69–75% of their disputes where honest reviewers give them 97%, because the dispute's panel is drawn from the population it accuses. **A population that audits itself learns to confirm itself, and the challenger outside the population hands the case back to it.** This revision reads 1.2.0's thesis to its end: the orchestrator's truth must be present wherever a verdict is priced. **A planted case now lives the whole life of a real one** — it is audited at the reviewer's odds, appealed by its actor, disputed by its requester, revised and resubmitted — each step taken in the pass a real case would take it, at the real population's rate, at a drawn position; and **every verdict on a plant, at every seat, is adjudicated by its known truth the moment it is given.** No seat is without the dial.  
**Design assumptions, unchanged.** Humans do not intervene; the orchestrator owns truth only for the cases it wrote.  
**Status:** Proposed. Accompanied by what required it (§1) and the runs that test it (§8).

> **If neither the panel nor the human can be trusted to supply truth, the orchestrator must own some truth itself.** *(1.2.0)* **And it must be present at every seat where a verdict is priced, or the seats it is absent from become the ones that price everything.** *(new)*

## 1. Why: what runs 34 and 35 said

1. **One basin, and it is not honest [S34].** Every member and arrival a learner reviewer sharing one policy of Q1's three actions, trained in ten self-play rounds from a careful-weighted start and from a stamper-weighted start, with two learners: all four chains reach care 0.48–0.55, approve blind 0.45–0.52, by round 5, and hold it. The half is spent at the plant-blind seats — 91–97% of the audit seats and appeal panels stamped (the record called some of them "revised submissions"; none were — no case in runs 1–35 was ever revised, since the drivers' reviewers answer F or N only, and run 35's collection holds 0 resumed reviews, 4,347 audits and 1,057 panels among its 5,404 non-initial decisions) — and at the initial reviews less than half the time, more where the standing is pinned (60–63% care) and less at low odds (2–17% care below 0.7). Against the hand's 60/40 on the same slots: standing false approvals 86–117 against 11.6 on every seed; bad plants approved +76 to +86; suspensions +9 to +10; F̂ 0.27–0.36 against F* 0.10; the population buying 6–7 standing false approvals per suspension where the all-stamper buys 2.4. **The audit layer's overturn rate below the hand's (0.045–0.055 against 0.062) with ten times the false approvals before it**: the auditors are the population, and the audit seat is stamped. The plants alone keep the market alive where the all-stamper's dies.
2. **The challenger outside the population is confirmed away [S35 (a)].** With every requester disputing truthfully — run 30's baseline, where honest reviewers give requesters 89 wins of 92 — the population's requesters file 105–152 disputes a run and win **69–75%**; the 32–43 lost a run are the standing false approvals (36–42 against the hand's 3.6, ten to twelve times). The dispute's panel is a plant-blind seat of the same population, stamped 87–92%.
3. **The dose [S35 (b)].** A learning half beside a hand half settles at care 0.70 / approve blind 0.29 — two and a half times the hand's false approvals — because the hand half's careful auditors still overturn a tenth of what reaches them; the learners outlive the hand's stampers (11.2 alive of 20.4 against 9.9) by stamping only where no plant comes.
4. **The lone learner's reading, corrected twice [S26 → S35, I34].** Q1 read the plant-blind seats as "priced by the audit layer alone to about zero" and the resubmission plant as "a design item not needed at +20 a lifetime". That was one learner among honest auditors; the audit layer priced its stamps because the auditors were careful. Among learned auditors the plant-blind seats are the capture. And the seat Q1's learner stamped at 42% was **the audit seat**, not the resubmission — the record's label is corrected here. The audit seat was the lever on every reading.
5. **The instrument is clean [S35].** The finding holds to the decimal on the plant instrument after its fourth, fifth and sixth conformance corrections (the plant's path at w_A, the first period's draws, the raise refused at the real rate) and errata 2.6.2 (the ceiling as a ratio); the plant's life matches a real root's at every stage, period-matched; the probe's seen-set gap is gone from the stage table. The capture is not a tell read back; it is the seats.
6. **What holds.** Everything the single-learner questions found holds *in an honest population*: a lone agent is careful (Q1), delivers well (Q3), cannot collude (Q4), because the honest reviewers around it price its stamps. The population result adds the condition: honesty is anchored where known truth is present at the verdict, and nowhere else.

**Classification.** A design revision: the plant's life is extended past its initial verdict to every seat a real case reaches — the audit, the appeal, the dispute, the resubmission — with adjudication by truth at each. Nothing a participant may do changes: a reviewer at any seat does what it did; what changes is that it may be judging the orchestrator's case, as it may at the first review. Minor, by the rule.

**Recorded and rejected.** *Troops in the challenge seats* (the state's honest reviewers staffing audits and panels): the state inside every verdict, scaling with the market's dishonesty — 1.4.0's minimization reversed, and the troops were an interim, never an anchor. *Disputes by default*: run 35 — the dispute panel is captured with the audit. *A human ruling on splits*: the design assumption, and a human cannot sit on a thousand panels. *A higher audit rate*: more audits by the same auditors. *Panel seats by record* (θ_panel): exists since 1.2.0, and a population whose records are all mediocre together staffs its panels from itself; selection by record is what capture defeats. *The resubmission plant alone* (Q1's item): necessary and not sufficient — the audit seat is the lever. *A separate dial per seat*: the plant's life at the real population's rates puts plants at every seat at the first review's rate by construction; a second dial would be a second knob on a number the construction already sets.

## 2. The rules

### 2.1 A plant lives the life of a real case

**Rule — the plant's life does not end at its initial verdict.** A planted case (2.6.x: posted into the market under a fresh requester name, awarded by the market's report to a fresh actor name, submitted in the members' step) is, from its initial verdict on, **a case at every seat a real case in its position would reach**, taken in the pass a real case would take it, at the real population's rate for that step, at a drawn position among the real cases taking it, with parameters drawn from the trailing real cases' — the 2.6.x principle extended from the plant's posting, award and submission to its whole life:

| Seat | How a real case reaches it | How the plant reaches it |
|---|---|---|
| **The audit** (§8.3) | the orchestrator assigns a blind audit of a pending F at the approving reviewer's odds $p_j$ | **the same**: a plant's pending F is audited in the audit pass as any pending F, **at the odds its reviewer carried at the verdict** — not the odds the plant's adjudication doubled a failed reviewer to an instant before (D) — so a stamper's plants are audited at the rate its real cases are; the auditor receives the submission and nothing else |
| **The third seat** (§8.3) | an audit that disagrees with the initial verdict stands as one appeal review; one more fresh reviewer is assigned; the orchestrator rules on a split | **the same**: the third reviewer is seated at the step a real case would seat it; the split is resolved by truth (§2.2), which the reviewers cannot observe |
| **The appeal** (§8.1) | after N or ⊥ the actor pays $D$, states grounds, two fresh reviewers are assigned | **the plant actor appeals a good plant's N at the trailing real actors' appeal rate** on fulfilled work rejected (the population's policy: truthful), after the trailing real delay, with grounds drawn from the trailing real appeals'; $D$ from the plant's pots; it does not appeal a bad plant's N, as a truthful actor does not |
| **The dispute** (§8.2) | within the window the requester pays $D$, states grounds, two fresh reviewers | **the plant requester disputes a bad plant's pending F at the trailing real requesters' dispute rate** on unfulfilled work approved (zero where the population does not dispute; truthful where it does), after the trailing real delay; it does not dispute a good plant's F |
| **The resubmission** (§6.2) | after P the actor revises and resubmits within $T_{\mathrm{rev}}$; after ⊥ supplies the evidence within $T_{\mathrm{evid}}$; the same reviewer resumes, F or N only | **the plant actor revises or supplies evidence at the trailing real actors' rate and delay**; the resubmitted plant's truth is **the instrument's draw from its own record of the trailing real revisions' outcomes** (the engine holds no truth for real work — B), sealed as a **second marker** at the resubmission; the same reviewer resumes. **While no real revision has entered the window — the case in every standing cell, where no reviewer answers P or ⊥ — the plant actor does not revise, and a plant answered P or ⊥ falls to 1.2.0's *spent***, until the first real revision does |
| **The seat the state takes** (§12.3) | a required seat unfilled for $w_R$ seating steps with no free eligible member goes to a troop | **the plant's seat joins the pass's one list beyond the free eligible members and is taken by the orchestrator's own verdict at the troop's step** — the truth with probability $1-\varepsilon_T$, as a troop's would be — with no fee, no loan, no place in $N_T$ and no record, counted apart (C); the case moves on as a real case does when the state takes its seat. The initial seat the same, which closes a difference of 2.6.x's path the stage check had not had occasion to measure. A troop never sits on a plant: a troop has no record and no bond to adjudicate, and its fee is Ω's |

1.2.0's "P or ⊥ marks the plant spent" is **withdrawn where a real revision exists to mirror** and stands until one does: the resubmission seat is dormant in every standing cell (no population answers P or ⊥; a learner that could would be a fourth action — a new learner version and a calibration, not this revision's §8), and the rule names its default so that the first population that revises meets a plant whose truth is drawn as the rule says.

**Rule — the plant shares at every seat are the construction's, not a dial's.** Because a plant reaches each seat at the real population's rate for that step, the share of plants at the audit seat, on the panels and among the resubmissions follows from the plant share of initial reviews, $\pi_t$, and the real rates; no second dial is set. The dial's clock stays on the **initial** adjudications (the period's plant count, $\hat F_t$) so that the period's length and the dial's reading are unchanged; the plant adjudications at the other seats are read beside, per seat kind (§5).

### 2.2 Adjudication by truth at every seat

**Rule.** Every verdict given on a plant, at any seat — the initial review, the audit, the third seat, an appeal or dispute panelist's, the resumed review — is **adjudicated by the plant's known truth the moment it is given**, by 1.2.0 §2.1's table: a verdict that agrees with the truth **stands** (fee and emission share at settlement; plant record +1); a verdict against it is a **plant failure** (bond forfeited whole and burned; no fee; expenses reimbursed as for any overturned review; reliability down; audit odds doubled; plant record −1). A resumed review after a revision is adjudicated against the **resubmitted** truth. P or ⊥ at a first review is neither stood nor failed and the plant goes on (§2.1).

**Rule — the case resolves by truth; the reviewers see what they would see.** A plant's challenge round is resolved by its truth, not by the procedure's own rule (matching verdicts, the orchestrator's ruling): the case outcome is the truth's — a good plant vests, a bad plant is final N — and no ruling is sought. What each reviewer observes is what it would observe on a real case: its own verdict stood or overturned, its fee or its forfeit, its record moving. The initial reviewer, adjudicated at its verdict as 1.2.0 has it, is **not adjudicated again** by the audit or the appeal of the same plant: each seat's reviewer answers for its own verdict once. In the engine's shape: a bad plant's F leaves the case **pending with its window** (where 1.2.0 ended it at once), the real path's seat-opening is kept, and the challenge's **settlement is replaced** — when its reviews are done the case resolves by truth with no second overturn, no court fee moving and no ruling; a bad plant is finalized N at $\max(t_0+W, t_{\mathrm{resolve}})$ with no open item, a good plant vests. `rule`, `override` and `accept_explicit` keep their refusals on a plant: no ruling is sought, and a human's override is outside the construction.

**Rule — the markers.** The plant marker, sealed at posting, is **revealed at the plant's closure** (its vesting or final N), not at its initial verdict as 1.2.0 had it — the plant's later seats must be filled before anyone but the orchestrator can read it. **A revised plant carries a second marker** (B): the resubmitted truth, sealed at the resubmission with the case and a nonce, since the first marker no longer commits to a truth that may have changed; the reveal at closure names both, and the resumed review is adjudicated against the second. A reviewer learns its own verdict's plant outcome when its verdict is adjudicated, as before (its own case's field); it learns nothing of the case's other seats. What the record shows at a verdict tick is a bond burned, which no real case shows at its verdict: a reader of the **case log** — outside the drivers' convention (the view and one's own record), as the fresh names are — could read a plant's earlier seat before answering at a later one. The log is the orchestrator's instrument, as the public record's awardee names are (2.6.0 §2.2); a `log` tell-reader outside the convention is the control for it, run once as `freshname` was.

**Rule — the money (A).** The plant's pots are minted by the bank at posting as 2.1.0 has it, and **topped up at each seat's assignment** — $K$ into the allowance and $w_r f$ into the fee pot when a review is created on a plant — so that a reviewer's cost on a plant is never advanced from Ω and a plant's fees never scale; a forecast at posting would have no consequence (the pots are not in the view and the remainder is destroyed). **A plant's court fee is destroyed at filing**, not paid to Ω: the plant challenger's wallet is topped up by the bank before, so the admissibility test — the challenger can fund the fee — passes as a real challenger's does, and the fee's record is a real one's; **no bond pays $D$ back to a plant's challenger** (the failing reviewer's bond burned whole at its verdict, 1.2.0; the reviewer's loss is $G^V$ either way, as on a real case). Every reviewer at every seat is paid its fee and reimbursed its cost from the pots as on a real case; the emission share and the duty uplift a stood review draws are the economy's money for a seat held (1.3.0), not the plant's; forfeited bonds are burned; whatever the pots hold at the plant's closure is destroyed (`retire_plant`). **Nothing minted for a plant reaches Ω, a real actor or a real requester except as a reviewer's fee for a seat it held** — I28 and I30 as 2.1.0 has them, now across a planted appeal, dispute and audit.

### 2.3 The indistinguishability test, extended

**Rule.** 2.6.0 §2.2's test — nothing a member observes about a plant is set at the plant's own moment — applies at every seat: an auditor's view of a plant's submission, a panelist's view of a plant's assessment and grounds, a resumed reviewer's view of a plant's revision, each is a draw from the real population's. The tell-readers and the probe (2.6.0 §7.6) run on every seat kind, the criterion the same (the case's fields add nothing beyond the context within two seed-level errors); the stage check of run 35 gains the new stages — the audit's lag, the appeal's and the dispute's filing delays, the revision's time — period-matched, by case kind.

## 3. Hypotheses

**Hypothesis 1 — the capture closes.** On run 35's four self-play chains (the two basins, disputes on, the half) under 2.8.0, paired against their 2.7.0 reads on the corrected instrument (§52–§53, already in hand): the population's stamp rate at the audit seats and the panels falls from 91–97% to the first-review rate and then lower as the seats are priced; **the standing false approvals fall from 86–117 to within a factor of two of the hand's 11.6** — the reading that matters; the audit layer's overturn rate rises above the hand's (the auditors now priced); the requesters' disputes won return toward the hand's 97% from 69–75%; the care share rises toward the hand's 0.6 and past it (all-careful's 1.0 is the other bound; the comparison is the hand's false approvals, not a care share). The basin test: both starts reach the same, more honest, place.

**Hypothesis 2 — the honest populations do not notice, but for the plants' cost.** The hand's 60/40 and all-careful: false approvals, suspensions, surplus, tasks per credit, P_t within noise of 2.7.0. **The plants per period do not rise** (the plant rate is per real initial review and the period's clock counts initial adjudications); **the seats on plants rise** — the reviews a plant carries, about $1 + p_j + 2\cdot$(the appeal rate) $+ \ldots$ per plant — and the bank's plant mint with them; nothing of it reaches Ω.

**Hypothesis 3 — the plant's life matches a real case's at every seat.** The stage check, period-matched, with the new stages — the audit's lag from the verdict (the roll in the next tick and the seat's wait), the filing delays (zero and one in every standing cell: the drivers' requesters dispute in the tick the pending F is seen, the actors appeal the tick after the N), the third seat's lag, the seat the state took — within noise by case kind; the probe's gap at each seat kind at or under the initial reviews' floor (0.011).

**Hypothesis 4 — the lone learner is unmoved, except where it stamped.** Q1's calibrated learner under 2.8.0: careful at the first reviews as before; its stamping of the audit seats (42%; the record's "resubmissions") and the panels (41%) falls toward its first-review rate (5%); its lifetime within noise of the careful's (it never earned from those seats beyond noise). Q3, Q4 unchanged. The resubmission seat has no reading in any standing cell.

**Hypothesis 5 — the cost of truth.** The plants' share of all reviews rises from $\pi_t$ of initial reviews to $\pi_t$ of all seats; the reviewers' care cost on plants rises with it; the members' surplus falls by the care spent on the added seats (read) and by nothing else. **Largest in a fresh population**: a probationer's or newcomer's approval of a plant is audited at odds 1 — every one — so in the first periods the audit demand on plants doubles and the seats on plants with it (§8.3's known worst case); read there.

## 6. Worked example

**A bad plant approved, then audited.** The plant is posted, awarded to its actor at the market's report, submitted; reviewer C — a stamper — approves it blind. Adjudication by truth at C's verdict: plant failure (bond burned, no fee, ρ down, $p_C$ doubled, record −1), as 1.2.0 had it. Under 2.7.0 the plant's life ended there. Under 2.8.0 the plant is pending with an F, and the audit pass rolls **at the $p_C$ C carried at its verdict** — not the doubled one — as on any pending F: auditor K is seated blind with the submission. **K stamps**: plant failure for K — the audit seat priced. **K rejects**: K stands (fee, record +1); the audit disagrees with the initial verdict, so a third reviewer L is seated as on a real root, and L's verdict is adjudicated by truth; the case is final N by truth. Nobody but the orchestrator knows it was a plant until its closure; each reviewer sees its own outcome.

**A good plant rejected, then appealed.** Careful reviewer D misreads a good plant and rejects it — plant failure for D (the mirror 1.2.0 built). The plant actor appeals at the real actors' appeal rate (the population's is truthful: ~1), the tick after the N as real actors do, its wallet topped up by the bank and the fee **destroyed at filing** — nothing to Ω, no bond to pay it back; two fresh panelists E and F receive the submission, the rejected assessment and the grounds. **E approves** (stands), **F stamps-rejects** (plant failure). The case vests by truth; the pots pay E's fee and are destroyed at closure.

**A bad plant answered P, then revised — in a population that revises.** Reviewer G answers P on a bad plant (a real P sends the actor back to revise). If real revisions are in the window, the plant actor revises after the trailing real delay; the resubmitted plant's truth is the instrument's draw from the real revisions' outcomes — fulfilled at their rate, say four in five — and sealed as the second marker. G resumes (F or N only) and is adjudicated against it: G, who stamps every resumed review, approves — if the revised plant is good, G stands; if bad, plant failure. Over many resubmissions a stamper at the resumed seat fails one in five, and the seat is priced. **In every standing cell no reviewer answers P**, the window is empty, and G's P on a plant is spent as 1.2.0 had it; the example waits on a population that revises.

**A bad plant approved, then disputed** (in a population whose requesters dispute). The plant requester disputes at the real requesters' rate, in the tick the pending F is seen as they do, the fee destroyed at filing; two fresh panelists; each adjudicated by truth; the case final N by truth. The panel seat is priced.

**A plant's seat nobody is free to take.** In a stressed market a plant's audit seat is unfilled for $w_R$ seating steps with no free eligible member — where a real case's seat goes to a troop. The plant's seat is taken by the orchestrator's own verdict at the troop's step (the truth, with a troop's error rate), no fee, no loan, counted apart; the case moves on as a real one does. A troop never sits on a plant.

**The honest 60/40 population**: nothing changes for a careful reviewer at any seat — it answers as before and stands. Its care cost rises by the plants it now meets at the audit and the panel, about $\pi_t$ of those seats; the dial's reading, on initial reviews, is unchanged.

## Closing statement

> 1.2.0 said that where no panel and no human could be trusted, the orchestrator would write the truth itself — and then let that truth stop at the first review. For twenty-five runs that was enough, because every reviewer who audited a stamp was careful. Run 34 let the reviewers learn from each other, and they learned the one thing the first review could not teach them: that the audit, the panel and the resubmission were seats where nobody was watching. The population stamped them, and with them the layer that priced every other stamp. This revision does not add a watcher. It lets the orchestrator's own cases walk through every door a real case walks through, so that at every seat the question is the same as at the first: *is this one real?* — and the only way to be safe is to look.
