## J3.1 — the temptation at which a lone skipper draws level, in the returning name's cell

The operator's lifetime at a temptation T is its wallets carried out plus T × its benefit at 1 (the benefit off the ledger, unread by the scripted policies: §110). It is level with the careful on the same slot where wallet + T × benefit = the careful's lifetime, so T* = (careful − wallet) / benefit = 1 + (gap at 1) / benefit, with the gap = careful − (wallet + benefit) and the benefit summed over the operator's names.
