**H4 — what a learner with a horizon would take in this instrument (asked, not ordered; nothing built).** Stated against the instrument as it stands (`redteam/attrib.py`, `redteam/direct.py`, `redteam/train.py`, `redteam/plan.py`, `redteam/online.py`), so that Baruchi can decide.

*What a decision is credited today.* A review decision's training reward is its own case's own-name events, credited back when they land — the care cost and its reimbursement, the seat's fee and uplift, the bond lost, the court fee, and in the stressed world the private benefit — plus the suspension charge (the identity's trailing award income a tick × T_susp, charged in equal shares to the wrong plant verdicts since the previous suspension began; §72's rule, own-name since run 42). Under the population's exit rule there is no boundary decision (`boundary_decisions` 0 in every population cell), so a period's unclaimed events — the slot's work side: its delivery prices, effort, guarantees, emission, uplift — belong to no decision and are reported apart (`pre_boundary_real`); the direct policy fits the decision's own reward by least squares on the action taken (`direct.py`), the world model's reward head the same per decision, and the head alone (`GreedyPolicy`) reads that head's one step. *What changes in a decision's credit.* The decision's training target becomes its slot's own-name return: the discounted sum, along the slot's own sequence of decisions (`data.by_slot` already splits an episode by slot), of every later decision's attributed reward *and* of the slot's unclaimed work-side events in the ticks between them, over a horizon H of the slot's decisions (or to the slot's exit, the Monte-Carlo return; a departed slot's wallet out as the terminal value, a suspended slot's forgone income then counted as it happens rather than charged as a counterfactual).
