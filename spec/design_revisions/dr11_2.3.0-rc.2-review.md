## The reading — a node's pots settle at the node's closure, and the single payout rule is per node

§4.3's single payout rule was written for one pair of pots per tree: expenses at each settlement from the root's allowance, fees from the root's pot, and **at the tree's closure** the pot's remainder settling the tree's expense debts before the refund. Under I36 every case has its own pair, so the rule applies **per node**: a case's reviews draw on that case's pots; a case's closure — when it and every case under it have settled, since a nested case may itself delegate (§9.1) — settles its own expense debts from its own fee pot and refunds the remainder to its requester, the parent; the root's pots serve the root's reviews alone and refund at the tree's closure as now. I19 (the pots) is amended from "for every root" to "for every case", I10's two post-vesting exemptions (a nested case's draw on the root's pots; the tree's closure) narrow to the case's own closure, and Ω's advance and claim path is per case. The 1.1.0 reading stays behind `nested_pots root` as the control, with I19 as it is. If the text means the nested pots to refund only at the **tree's** closure (the root's moment), say so; a node's own closure is the natural reading of "as a root's refund to its requester".
