## 7. One consequence found in implementation, for the reviewer

§4.3 has Ω advance a reviewer's reimbursement when the allowance is exhausted and pay its outstanding reviewer claims first in, first out on every inflow. Under the pool a fee inflow is locked until the close, so the advance and the claim are paid from what the pool excludes — the returns of capital, the retained balance, the pool's remainder at the close. In a world that starts with no float they wait for the close: two reviewer tests that pinned a service fee advancing and paying a claim at once (`test_review_06`, `test_r4_08`) are expected failures with that reason (30 in all); their principle holds at the close (`test_v22`). Ranking the reviewer claims before the pool was tried and does not save them — the advance, not the payment, is what the pool changes — so the text stands as written and the reading is in Appendix O. In every run of this study Ω's retained balance covered its advances from the second period on (reviewer claims outstanding read zero at every close). Whether a reviewer's reimbursement should wait for the state's rebate pool in the first period is the reviewer's call; nothing in the runs turns on it.
