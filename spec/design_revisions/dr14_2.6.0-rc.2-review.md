## One reading, for the record

**The deadline comes with the report, as the price does.** The engine sets a root's deadline at the award (`deadline = t_award + τ`, the driver's τ — the work ticks plus the nested room); until then `Case.deadline` is None. §2.1's "a deadline drawn from the trailing distribution of real roots' deadlines" at the posting therefore has nothing to attach to: when the market's selection reports `award(cid, member, B, τ)` on a plant, the engine takes **both** $B$ and $τ$ from the report — the plant then carries exactly the deadline a real root awarded that tick would carry, by construction rather than by sampling. The draw is needed only on the $p_T$ path (no bid by $w_A$), where a real root would get the troop's $τ_T$; the plant actor takes $τ_T$ there too. The time-to-submission stays the plant actor's draw from the trailing real deliveries' `t_submit − t_award`, bounded within $(0, τ]$. I will implement it so and record it in Appendix S; no change to the rule's content.
