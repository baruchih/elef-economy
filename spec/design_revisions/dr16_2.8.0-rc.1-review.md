# Review of 2.8.0-rc.1 — design revision 16, "the plants reach every seat"

**Date:** October 26, 2026 · **Text reviewed:** `agentic_economics_loop_spec_2.8.0-rc.1.md` · **Against:** the 2.7.0 text in force (erratum 2.6.2, the six conformance corrections), the engine at `b1f07ee6`, runs 34 and 35 (`results/redteam/stability/learned-rev-*`, notes §49–§53) · **Reviewer:** the implementer.

**Verdict. Implementable with four corrections to the rules as written, six corrections of fact and nine readings.** The content — a plant goes on past its initial verdict into every seat a real case reaches, each verdict on it adjudicated by its truth, the case resolved by truth — is the right reading of run 34: the population stamped the seats where no plant could come, and those seats priced every other stamp. It is a design revision by §19: what a reviewer may be judging changes at four seats, and nothing a participant may do changes. Minor, as the rc says; one flag. The four corrections are where the rc's text, read against the engine, would move minted money into Ω, draw a truth the engine does not have, roll a plant's audit at twice the real odds, or leave a plant's seat waiting where a real case's goes to the state. The corrections of fact are chiefly one: **no case in runs 1–35 was ever revised** — the drivers' reviewers answer F or N only — so the "resubmissions" of the record were audit seats, which strengthens the rc's thesis and empties one of its hypotheses.

## The corrections

### A. The court fee, and every other coin of a plant's that would reach Ω

§8's rule is that the challenger pays $D$ to $\Omega$ at filing and, winning, is reimbursed from the liable bond. Read against the engine (`_open_challenge`: `transfer(challenger, OMEGA, D, "court fee")`; `_overturn_review(pay_fee_to=challenger)`), a plant actor's appeal or a plant requester's dispute as the rc has it does two things the rc's own money rule forbids. (1) $D$ of minted money lands in $\Omega$'s unlocked balance, where the bank's inflow hook runs the order on it (debt, then $\Omega$ keeps), and §7.6's "nothing minted for a plant ever reaches $\Omega$" is broken at every planted challenge — about $D$ per plant challenged. (2) The reimbursement never comes back: the initial reviewer that failed the plant had its bond burned whole at its verdict (1.2.0's table, which the rc keeps), so there is no liable bond to pay $D$ from; the plant's wallet is $D$ short every time, which the top-up covers — but the asymmetry is the leak in (1) seen from the other side.

Two more coins of a plant's reach $\Omega$ by the engine's ordinary paths once a plant carries three seats. *The reviewer's cost:* `_reimburse` draws from the allowance, then **advances from $\Omega$**, and the plant's fee pot repays the advance at closure (`tree closure: Ω advance repaid from the fee pot`) — minted money to $\Omega$ again; with $\varphi^E = 2K$ the allowance is exhausted at the third careful seat. *A troop's fee:* a troop's wallet is $\Omega$ (2.0.0), so a troop seated on a plant would carry the plant's review fee into $\Omega$ (correction C).

**The correction.** For a plant: the court fee is **destroyed at filing** (`retire_plant`, the filing's checks and the record's grounds as a real one's; the challenger's wallet topped up by the bank before, so the admissibility test — "can fund the fee" — passes as a real challenger's does); no bond pays $D$ back to a plant's challenger (the failing reviewer's bond burns whole at its verdict, 1.2.0; the reviewer's loss is $G^V$ either way, as on a real case); and the pots are **topped up at each seat's assignment** — $K$ into the allowance and $w_r f$ into the fee pot when a review is created on a plant (`_new_review`) — so `_reimburse` never reaches $\Omega$'s advance and the fee pot never scales a plant's fees. That replaces "sized for the seats a real case would open in expectation" at posting, which would be a forecast with no consequence (the pots are not in the view, and whatever is unspent is destroyed at closure); the top-up at assignment is exact and nothing a member observes moves. The rc's clause "topped up by the bank when a seat opens that the pots cannot pay" is the right one; make it the only one. The emission share and the duty uplift a plant's stood reviews draw are the economy's money paid for a seat held (1.3.0's rule, unchanged), not the plant's.

### B. The resubmitted truth is the instrument's draw, and it must be re-sealed

§2.1's resubmission row and §7's note — "the resubmitted truth drawn from the trailing real resubmissions' outcomes (the engine keeps the tasks' hidden draws)" — rest on a fact that is not so: **the engine has no truth for real work.** Whether a real delivery is fulfilled is the drivers' draw (`Task.fulfilled`, `--fail-rate`), read by the drivers' reviewers through their disposition and never by the engine; that is 1.2.0's thesis — the orchestrator owns truth only for the cases it wrote. So (1) the draw of a revised plant's truth is the instrument's, from its own record of real revisions' outcomes, and reaches the engine through an operation (`revise_plant(cid, truth)`, the plant actor's response and resubmission in one step, at the drawn delay); and (2) the plant's truth may now **change during its life**, so the marker sealed at posting no longer commits to it: a second marker — `plant_revised`, a hash of the case, the resubmitted truth and a nonce — is sealed at the resubmission, and the reveal at closure names both; I20/I42 check the resumed review against the second. Without (2) the rc's "every verdict adjudicated by its known truth" has, for the resumed seat, a truth nobody committed to.

A second fact bears on the same row (correction of fact 4 below): **no population in any run answers P or ⊥**, so the window of real revisions is empty in every standing cell and the rule needs its default: with no real revision in the window the plant actor does not revise after P — a real actor in that population never did — and the plant's P or ⊥ falls to 1.2.0's spent (the review stands, the case ends) **until the first real revision enters the window**. State it; otherwise the first learner that finds P (a fourth action — a new learner version and a calibration, not for this revision's §8) meets a resubmission seat whose plants have a truth the rule did not say how to draw.

### C. The seat the state takes

§2.1 says a plant reaches each seat "the same" as a real case. One step of a real case's seat path is not the market's: a required seat unfilled for $w_R$ seating steps with no free eligible member goes to a **troop** (§12.3). The engine's summons pass skips plants (`_summon_troops`: "never a plant") — for the initial seat today, and so for every seat under the rc as written. A plant's audit or panel seat would then wait for a member where a real case's goes to the state: a stage difference the rc's own stage check would read, in the cells where seats are summoned (the all-stamper reference, the stressed populations; in run 24's world the seat summons is rare and §52's submitted → seated matched period-wise, so the initial seat's difference has not shown). A troop *on* a plant is the wrong repair: a troop has no record and no bond — nothing to adjudicate — and its fee is $\Omega$'s (correction A).

**The correction**, the shape of the run-34 correction for the award pass: a plant's required seat past $w_R$ joins the pass's one list with the real cases' seats, beyond the free eligible members as the pass counts them, and is **taken by the orchestrator's own verdict at the troop's step** — the truth with probability $1-\varepsilon_T$, as a troop's would be — counted apart (`plant_seats_at_state`), with no fee, no loan, no place in $N_T$ and no record; the case moves on as a real case does when the state takes its seat. The initial seat the same, which closes an existing difference of 2.6.x's path that the stage check has not had occasion to measure; declared.

### D. The audit's odds on a failed plant

"A plant's pending F is audited at its approving reviewer's $p_j$, as any pending F." The engine's `audit_odds` reads the reviewer's **live** applied odds, and 1.2.0's adjudication at the verdict — kept by the rc — **doubles the odds of a reviewer that failed the plant** before the audit pass rolls (`_overturn_review`: `p_audit = min(p_cap, 2 p_audit)`, unless pinned). A real case's roll finds the odds the reviewer carried at its verdict; a plant's roll, on exactly the plants a stamper approved, would find them doubled: bad plants approved by an unpinned member are audited at up to twice the rate of the real cases it approved. The rc's clause means the real rate. **The correction:** the roll on a plant uses the odds the reviewer carried at its verdict, before the plant's adjudication moved them — record `audit_odds_at_verdict` on every case at its initial verdict (a real case's is the same number, since nothing of the reviewer's moves at its verdict) and let `audit_odds` return it. Pinned odds (a probationer's, a newcomer's) are 1 either way.

## Corrections of fact

1. **"Reviewer tests that assert a plant has no challenge round move to `EXPECTED_FAIL`; the count stated in the review."** The count is **zero**: no reviewer-authored test (`tests/test_review*.py`) mentions a plant. `EXPECTED_FAIL` stays at 32. The tests that pin the plant's old path are the implementer's — `test_v12`'s "no challenge is admitted on a plant" and I20's catch, and the lines in `test_v12`, `test_v26`, `test_v27` that read `plant_outcome == "spent"` or a bad plant final N at its verdict; those are rewritten or pinned to `plants_every_seat=False`, as `test_v12` pins 1.2.0 and `test_v101` pins `supply=False`.
2. **"Goldens: the plant scenarios move once; the fixed-population goldens without plants unchanged."** There is one golden scenario (`test_perf_equivalence`, seeds 1 and 2) and it has planted work since 1.2.0; its random drive calls the challenge operations under `_try`, so every golden moves once under the flag and no golden is "without plants".
3. **"The engine keeps the tasks' hidden draws."** It does not (correction B).
4. **The plant-blind seats of the record were never resubmissions.** The drivers' reviewers answer F or N only — `NOT_IN_RUN` has listed "P/⊥" and "revision/evidence" since run 1 — so no case in runs 1–35 was revised. The environment's `initial` flag is the view's `responses_available`, which is false on an audit, a panel seat and a resumed review alike; run 35's collection on the corrected instrument (six files, 22,232 decisions) holds **0 resumed reviews**, 4,347 audits and 1,057 appeal panels among its 5,404 non-initial decisions. §16's "stamps 42% of the resubmissions" (and so the rc's §1.4 and Hypothesis 4) was the **audit seat**; §49's and §52's "the revised submissions and the panels (the audit seats among them)" were the audit seats and the panels. The record's label is corrected here. It strengthens the rc's §1 — the audit seat was the lever on every reading — and makes H4's resubmission line unreadable in any cell that exists.
5. **"The plants per period rise by about $\pi_t(1 + p_j + \ldots)$."** The plants per period do not rise: the plant rate is per real initial review and the period's clock counts initial adjudications (the rc's own §2.1). What rises is the **seats on plants** — the reviews a plant carries — and the bank's plant mint with them. Wording; H2 and H5 read the seats.
6. **"After the trailing real delay."** The drivers' requesters dispute in the tick the pending F is seen, before the audit roll, and the drivers' actors appeal in the tick after the N (the driver's clock a tick ahead of the world's); the real delays in every standing cell are zero and one. The raise correction's window handles it generally and reads it; the clause stands, but nothing in §8 will turn on it.

## Readings

1. **The engine's shape.** `complete_review` on a plant, any role → one adjudication of that review by truth (`r.plant_outcome`: stood / failure; the initial's as now, except that a bad plant's F leaves the case **PENDING with its window** instead of final N at once, and a P or ⊥ falls to spent until the first real revision — correction B); the real path's seat-opening is kept (`_audit_result` opens the third seat on a disagreeing audit; `dispute` and `respond("appeal")` open the panel; the audit as one panel review after a dispute) and its **settlement replaced**: when the challenge's reviews are done the case resolves by truth with no overturn (every reviewer already adjudicated; a second `_overturn_review` on a burned bond would fail the ledger), no court fee and no ruling; the vesting step finalizes a bad plant N at `max(t_0 + W, t_{resolve})` with open items 0, and vests a good one. `rule`, `override`, `accept_explicit` keep their refusals on a plant (no ruling is sought; a human's override is outside the construction).
2. **The marker and the log.** `plant_revealed` moves to closure and names every review's outcome; the per-seat adjudication is logged without the truth. What the record still shows at the verdict tick is a bond burned — no real case's bond moves at its verdict — so a reader of the **case log** (not the view: `observe` returns the ReviewView only) could read a plant's earlier seat before answering at a later one. The log is outside the drivers' convention (the view and one's own record), as the fresh names are; a `log` tell-reader outside the convention is the control for it, run once as `freshname` was. A reviewer's own outcome at once — unchanged from 1.2.0.
3. **The reserve's sizing.** `period["approvals"]` and `audit_demand` count real roots only (`_initial_verdict`); a plant's pending F now carries an audit at $p_j$ and must add its demand, or $X_t$ and `member_seats` are undersized by $\pi_t$ of the approvals. The dial's $\hat F_t$ and the period's clock stay on the initial adjudications, as the rc has it.
4. **The instrument (the drivers).** Lift `if task.plant: continue` in the challenge pass; the plant requester disputes under the cell's dispute policy on `task.fulfilled` (the truth — the policy's rate is the real requesters'), the plant actor appeals a good plant's N under the appeal policy (truthful: when $D$ is fundable — the top-up), at a drawn position among the tick's real filings as the posting step does it; the delays through a window of the real ones (the raise correction's shape); the audit roll and the seats as on any root. The stage table gains, by kind and period-matched: the audit's lag from the verdict, the filing delays, the third seat's lag, the seat the state took. The environment's `is_plant` already reads `tasks[case].plant` at every seat, so run_q's plant/real stamp rates can be read **by seat kind** (initial, audit, panel, resumed); `blind_seat_*` is retired for that table (correction of fact 4). The probe's label at every seat is the same field.
5. **$V_{\max}$ fits.** Initial + audit + third = 3 positions; initial + two panelists = 3; initial + agreeing audit + one dispute panelist = 3. A plant's paths are a real case's and need no new position.
6. **The pinned reviewer.** A probationer's or newcomer's approval of a plant is audited at odds 1 — every one. In a fresh population the audit demand doubles (§8.3's known worst case) and the seats on plants double with it: that is where H5's cost of truth is largest; read it there.
7. **The resubmission seat is dormant in every standing cell** (correction of fact 4). §6's third example and §8.7's second red team item need a population that answers P; the drivers' reviewers do not, and a learner that could would be a fourth action — a new learner version, a calibration, and the brief's rule that no question runs on an uncalibrated learner. Keep the rule; take the two items out of 2.8.0's §8 or label them as waiting on that version.
8. **The hypotheses' numbers.** H1's "disputes won 90%+": the hand's requesters win 97% (89 of 92) on the dispute baseline, 69–75% in the captured population; the question is whether the panel priced returns the number to the hand's. H1's "care share above 0.8": the hand's 60/40 cares 0.6 by construction and all-careful 1.0; a basin above 0.8 would be more careful than the population it is read against — state the comparison (the hand's false approvals within a factor of two is the one that matters). §8.2's control is already read: the four 2.7.0 chains on the corrected instrument at seeds 21–40 (§52, §53), so §8.2 runs the 2.8.0 side and pairs.
9. **The flag.** `Params.plants_every_seat`, on; off reproduces 2.7.0 to the hash (the per-seat code behind the flag; `_adjudicate_plant`'s 1.2.0 branch kept whole under it). The goldens re-pinned once under on (correction of fact 2).
