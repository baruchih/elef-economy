# Review of 1.3.0-rc.1 — design revision 3, "inspection is membership"

**Date:** October 17, 2026 · **Text reviewed:** `agentic_economics_loop_spec_1.3.0-rc.1.md` · **Against:** the 1.2.0 text in force, the engine at `d1e975c`, run 4 (`agentic_sim/results/studies/1.2.0-clock/`) · **Reviewer:** the implementer.

**Verdict.** The mechanism answers run 4's finding at its root — the award, not the fee, is the money channel, so the gate is on awards — and it is correctly classified. rc.1 is not implementable as written: the readmission test is stated two ways and the worked example follows the wrong one (1), the duty cap as an invariant contradicts "no actor leaves mid-review" and its own panel floor at small memberships (2), and three rules the engine must hold are implied but not stated (3–5). All are short. The 1.2.1 sentence (Appendix A) is applied to the text in force today, as the revision directs, independently of this candidate's fate.

## Must fix before rc.2

1. **Readmission is stated two ways, and §6 follows the wrong one.** §2.1 says the record is untouched by suspension and 1.2.0 §11.2 says it persists. §2.2 then readmits when "its record passes θ_duty over at least n_min new plant outcomes since the suspension began". On the persisting trailing record, a full stamper that approved four bad plants and then rejects four has â = (4+1)/(8+2) = 0.5 — passing, as the Consequence's "about four in a row" says. §6 computes the same case as (0+1)/(4+2) = 0.17 and concludes "panel-eligible at once since 0.17 ≤ 0.2". Only the new outcomes give 0.17; the record in force gives 0.5, which is not panel-eligible (θ_panel = 0.2) until the four approvals roll out of the trailing window of 20. Choose: (a) readmission and panel eligibility on the trailing record, ≥ n_min new outcomes required — then §6 reads "readmitted at about tick 660; panel-eligible once the window has rolled, after about twenty more bad plants"; or (b) readmission on the new outcomes alone — then say so, and say which record the panel tier reads. I recommend (a): one record, one meaning; the slower return of panel seats to a reformed stamper is the right price, and §2.2's "panel seats return separately, at θ_panel, as in 1.2.0" already says it.

2. **I23's cap cannot be a standing per-tick predicate as written.** "On-duty members ≤ ⌈E_t/2⌉" fails on a correct engine twice over: E_t falls when a member is suspended mid-tick, and a member above the new cap may hold an open review, which 1.2.0 §11.2 says it completes before leaving. State it as I21 was restated: no member is *seated* while on-duty members ≥ ⌈E_t/2⌉; a member above the cap leaves at the completion of its active review. And the floor conflicts with the cap at small memberships: "never fewer than V_max − 1 members" (two, for one panel) against ⌈E_t/2⌉ = 1 at E_t = 2. Say which yields. I recommend the floor, bounded by E_t: a market with two honest members seats both and has nobody free — which is what it is.

3. **The reserve's sizing must use the applied odds, not m p̄.** 1.2.0 §12.2 sizes audit demand as m_t p̄ times the period's approvals. Under rc.1 a probationer's approvals are audited at 1 and a newcomer's at 1 until its bad-plant record is determined, so the audit demand is Σ over the period's initial approvals of the approving reviewer's applied odds, not a population mean. The member seats of §2.3 ("the period's expected audit and challenge reviews") are that sum times d̄, floored at V_max − 1. One sentence in §2.3.

4. **Turns need their refill order.** 1.2.0 §12.1 rotates by turns, replacement before release. rc.1 states the fill order for the reserve as a whole but not for a seat a turn vacates. Say: a seat vacated by a turn is refilled in the order of §2.3 for the kind of seat it was — a member seat by a member, an initial seat by a probationer first.

5. **Netted plant capital changes two invariants and one settlement path; the text should say so.** A plant that ties up no award and no guarantee has no escrow and no guarantee lock, so I15 (every live obligation has a lock record of exactly its size) exempts a plant's guarantee, I12 keeps B and G as frozen nominals that bind nothing, and vesting a good plant pays no price and releases no guarantee — the plant actor's wallet is notional. State it under I21's amendment. The engine's `test_v12` figures ("35 moves from Ω"; "Ω's net cost 0") become "15 moves"; those are implementer tests and are revised.

## Smaller

- **Probation pays a stamper a little; the award is what it loses.** On probation a stamper's approvals are all audited: at b = 0.2 and 95% auditor accuracy it loses a bond (5) on 19% of reviews and earns f = 2 plus uplift on the 80% that stand, about +1.2 a review net at m = 2. Probation is not loss-making for it; what bites is the award it cannot win. Consistent with Hypothesis 1 — worth one sentence so nobody expects probation itself to deter.
- **The newcomer pin in a fresh population.** When a run starts every actor is undetermined, so every approval is audited and every auditor is itself an undetermined newcomer; a stamper auditing a stamper agrees. The pin buys little until the first records form (about 50 reviews each), and it doubles the review demand in the first period, so X_t will size up at once. §8 says it measures the cost; name this part of it.
- **A careful reviewer's probation.** "A plant failure suspends it again at once" is any failure, not a failing record: a careful probationer at ε = 0.05 fails one of its four needed bad plants with probability 19% and restarts the clock. Acceptable, but say it is intended; the alternative (re-suspend only when the record fails) is one word.
- **Duty-fund accounting.** Add the fund to §10.4's flows (in: (m_t − 1) ω₀ B per award; out: uplifts by role weight per batch; a wallet, so I1 holds) and to the plant case: a plant's stood review settles in the same batch and draws uplift like any other.
- **1.2.1.** The Appendix A sentence is applied today to the text in force as `spec/agentic_economics_loop_spec_v1_2_1.md` (patch: wording of a decision already made; 1.2.0 to history), so the in-force text no longer carries an "implementer's reading" note. 1.3.0 subsumes it on acceptance.
- **§2.6's m_max sweep.** Run 4's dial-off cell (plants at π₀, m = 1) already cut false approvals 650 → 257 under drift; the sweep against 2 is the right question, and Ω₀ against 100 follows from §2.5.
