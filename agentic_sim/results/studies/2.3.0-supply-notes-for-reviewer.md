## In one paragraph

**Supply finds its level, and the level is the ratchet's.** At 60/40 all twenty candidates arrive in the first two hundred ticks, review as probationers, and fifteen are admitted at a mean of 310 ticks; every stamper departs within six hundred ticks (its earnings fall to zero once suspended), the dearest careful incumbents follow, and the market ends with 19 members where run 15 had 8 — the price at 22.9 against 32.9, the state's share at 0.7% against 39%, the honest earning 1,237 real against 1,928. But the price does not go to cost: it dips to 18 as the admitted cohort bids, then climbs back to 22.5 as the free members settle at about nine and their margins ratchet to the constant run 13 found — the same 22.5 the twenty-honest cell gave with no entry at all. **Entry does not relieve inspection first; it relieves it until the arrivals are admitted, then takes it away:** reviews waiting are flat while the probationers carry the initial seats and rise once they become members and deliver. **Delegation runs where the spread is wide and the parents can afford it, and the children mostly refuse the price:** at σ 0.8 the dearest tenth post nested requests on a twentieth of their awards, three quarters go unawarded within T_wait because the children's ratchet bids exceed the parent's saving, and the parents self-execute; where a child is found the gain from trade is 3–4; brokers are the dearest two or three members with 5% of the market; a stamping parent approves bad child work and a careful parent's wrongful rejection is overturned on the child's appeal at the panel's rate — the chain holds in both directions. **Under the 1.1.0 reading the parents delegate 2.4 times as much**, because the root pays for the child's inspection. **Selection shows at the wide spread with entry:** the members' median cost falls from 8 to 6.4 as the dear depart (median 9.3) and the cheap arrive (7.2) — and the price rises with the spread anyway (21.8 / 22.5 / 23.5), because the price follows the free members' count, not their cost. Honesty is unmoved everywhere.

## 5. For the decision

1. **The level supply finds is the strategy's constant.** Entry takes the price to 22.5 and no further, because nine free members at the margin rule's decay is where awards per free member meet the constant — run 13's cliff from the other side. More supply than the demand model can occupy does not lower the price; it idles. That is a reading on the margin rule as the members' strategy, not on the rules — recorded for 2.4.0's §8 as the thing a bidding strategy would have to change.
2. **Entry's inspection relief ends with admission.** Hypothesis 2 assumed arrivals stay on the review seat; they leave it the day they are admitted, and the stampers who sat on duty depart. Whether the duty cap should rise with an all-honest list, or whether the rule is right as it is (members deliver, probationers inspect), is a §12.1 question the run raises.
3. **The pool's size is the lever's length.** Twenty candidates were all in by the second window; the run never saw a candidate stay out for want of earnings. §8.5's knobs (the reservation, the arrivals a tick, the float) and a larger pool are where "supply finds its level" is actually tested.
4. **Delegation's scale is the children's bids.** A child bids with the ratchet's margin; the parent pays at most its saving. Three quarters of nested requests die at $T_{\text{wait}}$. The gain from trade is there (3–4 a case) and the chain holds; the volume waits on a bidding strategy that prices a child's work at the child's cost.
5. **Nothing in the rules.** The parent's pots did what they were moved for: without them the parents delegate 2.4 times as much on the root's deposit.
