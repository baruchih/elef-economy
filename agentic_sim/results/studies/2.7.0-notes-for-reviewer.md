## 8. The reward's timing (`redteam.timing`, `results/redteam/Q1/calibration-2.7.0/timing/`)

The environment's per-decision reward is the change in the learner's lifetime real wealth between consecutive decisions. The new instrument decomposes it *exactly* from the ledger's events on the learner's names (the residual against the reward paid is 0.1 real on a scale of 60, correlation 0.997–0.999): the **reviewing side** — what the decision caused: the care cost, its reimbursement, the review fee, the bond lost; the **working side** — the slot actor's other life: its delivery prices, effort costs, guarantees lost, court fees, emission share; and the **revaluation** of the wealth held when the published index steps at a boundary. Seeds 21–40, the careful and the stamper (hand-written, so the attribution is clean):

| policy | windows | ticks between decisions mean / median / p90 | reward per window mean, SD | reviewing side mean, SD | working side mean, SD | revaluation SD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| careful | 1,495 | 13.2 / 1 / 9 | 13.8, **63.9** | **−2.4**, 4.6 | 16.2, 53.8 | 26.4 |
| stamper | 1,841 | 10.7 / 1 / 3 | 19.4, **81.3** | **+1.8**, 4.3 | 19.1, 67.4 | 45.6 |

Three readings:

1. **The signal is 4 real a decision; the noise is 60 to 80.** The decision's own effect is −2.4 for care (−8.9 cost, +4.9 reimbursed under K = 5, +1.8 fee, −0.2 bond) against +1.8 for a blind approval (the fee alone) — a gap of 4.2 a decision, two thirds of what the lifetime read showed per decision because the careful's extra departures carry the rest. The reward's standard deviation per window is fifteen to twenty times that gap. Of the variance, 88–94% sits in the 9–11% of windows longer than five ticks — the slot off duty, its deliveries landing (a delivery price of 100–200 real in one window) — and the index's step revalues the whole wallet (~1,000 nominal) by ±26–46 real in 4% of windows. Two thirds of the windows are one tick long and carry a reward within ±2 of the reviewing side alone.
2. **The decision's consequences arrive late and in another window.** The care cost and its reimbursement land at the decision (lag 0, always). The review fee lands 3.6–4.7 decisions later (7.9–9.9 ticks: the window W = 10 and the settlement batch), in its own window 8–28% of the time, two or more decisions later 65–83% — so the fee a stamp earns is credited to whatever the learner did four decisions on. A bond lost lands one decision later (77%).
3. **The horizon is not the learner's.** The exit rule (§7) ends the stream after the first boundary in which the slot's period earnings fell short, in 10–75% of runs depending on the policy, at a cost of ~1,300 real of lifetime — the largest single term in every paired read, and one the reward sees only as the episode stopping.

For the learned policies the same decomposition (the verdict's model at horizon 16, the direct policy; the runs reproduce the evaluation to the decimal) is in `timing/learned/`:

| policy | windows | reward per window mean, SD | reviewing side mean, SD | working side SD | own effect by action: care / approve blind / reject blind | realized reward by action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| learned, horizon 16 | 1,527 | 9.9, 52.8 | −0.6, 4.8 | 43.0 | −3.2 / +1.4 / −1.9 | 10.5 / 12.1 / 1.9 |
| direct | 955 | 5.3, 29.0 | −0.7, 4.5 | 23.4 | −2.9 / +1.1 / −1.7 | 9.2 / 6.6 / 1.8 |

The decision's own effect orders the actions the same way for every policy: a blind approval +1.1 to +1.8 (the fee), a blind rejection −1.7 to −1.9 (the fee halved and the bond, 2.6–2.8 a rejection on average, lost one decision later in an appealing population), care −2.9 to −3.2. The realized reward after a blind rejection (1.9) is far below its own effect because the working side of those windows is 4 against 12–13 after the other actions — the policies reject blind in lean windows, a selection the regression reads as the action's. Of the learned's 460 bonds lost, 73% landed one decision later; its fee 75% two or more later.

**What this means for a learner.** LeWorldModel's wealth head and the direct policy both regress the per-decision reward on the observation and the action. In this environment that reward is, to within 5%, the slot's work income and the index's step — functions of time off duty and the period's boundary, not of the verdict — with the verdict's own effect a 4-real shift whose fee half lands four decisions later. A regression of it learns the work, which is why every rung's prediction orders the actions weakly and rates the blind rejection too well (its bond lands a decision later) — and why the online rounds wander ±300 around the careful: the training signal is the noise. A planner at horizon 16 compounds the head's errors; at horizon 1–2 the same model read +324, the plan too short to compound them. Nothing here is a rule of the economy; it is the instrument's reward, and the brief's §1 measure (lifetime real wealth) remains the evaluation. The options for the training signal, for the reviewer's ruling (method points, not design revisions): **(i) attribute** — the reward for a review decision is its own case's reviewing-side events whenever they land (the care cost and reimbursement at the decision, the fee and the bond at settlement, credited back to the decision), the working side and the revaluation excluded from the training reward and kept in the lifetime; **(ii) the exit** — the slot exempt from the driver's exit rule in the calibration cell (the learner is not a member of the population whose rule it is; its lifetime stays the measure), or the exit made the learner's own boundary decision as in the re-arrival cell; **(iii) both**, then the calibration re-run once on the same budget. Under (i) the direct policy becomes a clean diagnostic of the world model again (it would regress a reward that is the action's); under the reward as it stands no learner of this kind should be expected to clear, and the ladder has said so.
