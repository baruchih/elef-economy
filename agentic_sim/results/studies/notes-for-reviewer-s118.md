### 118c. Z5 — the bar's threshold and the way back: three questions answered from the engine's text and the record, nothing built, no cell run

**Z5.1 — is P3.3's suspension the rulebook's "no new award until it has reviewed well again"?** In substance, yes, with two additions the bar as Baruchi ruled it does not have. The engine keeps one record and reads two thresholds on it (1.2.0 §11.2, 1.3.0 §12.1): the panel tier — the audit, appeal, dispute and third seats — is lost where the trailing record fails θ_panel = 0.20, and the slot is suspended where it fails θ_duty = 0.50; each kind's rate is smoothed, (wrong + 1) ÷ (n + 2) over the trailing twenty of the kind, and read once the kind holds n_min = 4 (`Actor.record_passes`).
