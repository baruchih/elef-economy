## Three points the settlements raise

**A. Clearing the record on return (§2.2) undoes Hypothesis 2.** On return from suspension "its record is cleared and it is treated as a newcomer", and an undetermined record "is eligible for both" tiers. A full stamper is then suspended for 100 ticks and eligible again for about 50 reviews — about 250 ticks at run 3's ≈ 200 reviews per actor per 1,000 ticks — until four more bad plants re-determine it. It is eligible roughly 70% of the time, on panels included. At 10/90 the reserve refills from returning stampers with clean slates and the panels stay stamper-majority at about 70% strength, indefinitely; the "shortage" of Hypothesis 2 never settles and the alarm has no better actors to seat than the ones it just removed. The fix is one sentence: **the plant record persists through suspension; T_susp is the interval after which the record is re-checked; an actor whose record still fails is not readmitted.** A full stamper at â = 0.83 cannot pass without rejecting bad plants (five rejections from 4/4 give (4+1)/(9+2) = 0.45), so it is out until it changes behaviour, which is the actuator Hypothesis 2 needs. "Newcomer on return" then means only the audit odds (p = 1). With this the eligibility shortfall at 10/90 is a stable state the record shows, as Hypothesis 2 says, not a 350-tick cycle.

*Proposed wording for §2.2:* "An actor whose record exceeds either threshold is suspended from duty for T_susp ticks. Its plant record persists; at the end of the suspension it is readmitted only if its record passes, and otherwise remains suspended for a further T_susp. On readmission its audit odds restart at p_start."

**B. Two rules for a newcomer's audit odds.** §2.4: p_start = 1.0 under the §8.3 rule, so 0.5 after five stood and the floor after about fifteen. §2.2, third bullet: an undetermined record "is audited at p = 1 until its record forms" — about 50 reviews for the bad-plant record and about 160 for the good-plant record (good plants are a quarter of plants). These disagree and the engine can hold only one. Recommendation: keep §2.4, a default under §8.3 that needs no new rule, and strike the clause from §2.2's bullet (an undetermined record is eligible for both tiers, full stop). If the hold at p = 1 is wanted, state it as a rule tied to the bad-plant record only, since audits check approvals.

**C. Eligibility is a property at assignment; the records move mid-review.** I21 says "every challenge-panel seat is held by a panel-eligible actor" and "every actor on duty is duty-eligible". An actor's â changes whenever one of its plants is adjudicated, so an actor holding an assignment can cross a threshold mid-review, and the per-tick predicate as written would fail on a correct engine. Two sentences settle it: **eligibility is checked at assignment and at rotation; an actor that becomes duty-ineligible completes its active review, whose verdict is adjudicated as usual, and leaves duty when it completes, whether or not a replacement is available.** The last clause matters: §12's "if no replacement is available the outgoing actor stays" is what the engine's rotation does today, and suspension must override it or the negligent actor is kept to make the number, which §2.2 forbids.

*Proposed I21:* "1 ≤ m_t ≤ m_max; each step within [0.9, 2]; π_t ≤ π_max; X_t ≥ V_max + 1; the reserve holds min(X_t, eligible) actors; no assignment is made to an actor ineligible for that tier at the time of assignment; no actor remains on duty past the completion of its active review once duty-ineligible; the eligibility and plant shortfalls are counted."

## Smaller

- **The period counts adjudications, not settlements.** "50 settled plants" would read the reserve of a window ago: a plant is adjudicated at its verdict and settles W = 10 or more ticks later. Write "50 adjudicated plants, or 200 ticks".
- **r̂ forms slowly.** At β_P = 0.75, n_min = 4 good plants take about 160 reviews, roughly 800 ticks at the defaults. Acceptable, since appeals already expose false rejecters at a ≈ 0.98, but say so.
- **θ_panel = 0.2 bites careful reviewers at the break-even ε.** With n_P = 20, a careful reviewer at ε = 0.05 is off panels about 2% of the time (four or more failures in twenty); at ε = 0.10 about 13%. Not a change; a figure for the §8 runs to report, since it is a cost of the mechanism on the honest side.
- **Appendix A initialization:** the condition |A₀|·50 + 20 ≤ M₀ becomes + 300. The simulation's M₀ is unaffected.
- **Two orchestrator identities, one wallet.** The engine gives each id a wallet and refuses an award whose actor is the case's requester. Implementation reading, for the record: the plant requester and plant actor are two system wallets, funded by transfer from Ω at the plant's creation and swept back to Ω at tree closure; `create_plant` builds the case in the submitted state without the award path; I1 and I17 hold as written, and "Ω's unlocked balance" is Ω's wallet before the transfer. If a different reading is intended, say so.
