# Agentic-OS — the economic loop

A rulebook for a task → verification → dispute → settlement loop among autonomous actors, and a reference
implementation that encodes it with no strategies.

- **Start with `HANDOFF.md`** — status, history, what is open, and the proposed next step.
- `CLAUDE.md` — working conventions and the change discipline (read automatically by Claude Code).
- `spec/agentic_economics_loop_spec_v2_2_0.md` — the baseline in force (2.2.0: design revision 10, the rebate from income, accepted October 19, 2026 — the clock's claim paid at the period's close from the period's fee pool pro rata, the rest forgone and read; on 2.1.0, the bank, accepted the same day — a second account that alone creates and destroys credits, mints by rule, publishes the index once a period and lends the state a summons's shortfall repaid from its own return; the state at its expected cost, its debt repaid from every inflow, the wall, refusals told; on 2.0.0, the first major version: floating prices, the price level indexing every nominal, no backing, the state's deficit read in public, the capacity parameter; on top of 1.7.1's per-request summons, 1.7.0's the state's earnings, 1.6.0's auto-scale, 1.5.0's the reserve is sized to the need, 1.4.0's the orchestrator's troops, 1.3.0's inspection is membership, 1.2.0's the loop's own clock and 1.1.0's two pots; the accepted revision texts and the implementer's reviews of their candidates are in `spec/design_revisions/`; superseded texts in `spec/history/`).
- `reviews/` — ten external reviews, one per version, with an index.
- `agentic_sim/` — engine, invariants, tests, drivers, results. `cd agentic_sim && python3 run_tests.py` → 282 passed, 0 failed, 30 expected failures.
- `docs/` — text export of the `_line` map of the loop.
