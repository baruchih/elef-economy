# K3 — a skipper that loses no time: the slots' ticks, income and benefit by standing, and the counterfactual of a claiming slot earning at its good-standing rate for a careful slot's good-standing ticks

Each world's claiming (or dosing) group against its careful group, a slot (each seed's groups averaged a slot, then ± SE over seeds). `good` a member in good standing; `down` suspended or on probation; `gone` the run's ticks the slot was not in place. The income is the wallet's change tick by tick at each tick's index, net of the loan (so it is the lifetime's wallet up to the index's drift and the genesis wallet); the benefit the private benefit. The rate is (income + benefit) a tick in good standing. The counterfactual: the claiming slot's good-standing rate × the careful group's good-standing ticks a slot; its gap to the careful slot's lifetime (wallet + benefit) is the last column, negative where the skipper would be ahead.
