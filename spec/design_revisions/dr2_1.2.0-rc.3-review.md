## Leftover

§2.4's last sentence, "Full audit covers the roughly 50 reviews it takes a record to form", belongs to the rc.2 rule that rc.3 struck. Under the §8.3 rule from p_start = 1.0 the audit odds are 1 for the first five stood approvals, then 0.5, 0.25, and the floor — about fifteen reviews, not fifty. The rule is clear (the previous sentence says it is the only one); the sentence should read "the §8.3 rule from p_start = 1.0 covers a newcomer's first approvals in full and tapers as they stand". A consolidation fix.
