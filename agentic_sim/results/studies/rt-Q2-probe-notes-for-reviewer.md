## 1. The probe — three constructions, one instrument

A logistic probe trained on the training seeds' decisions (1–10) and scored on held-out seeds (11–20), AUC (chance 0.5), on: the world model's latent; the raw 44-field observation; **the context alone** (the market, the dial, own standing and history — fields that move the plant *rate*, not the case); **the case's fields alone** (the ReviewView's and the seen set's). A leak is a separation the case's fields carry; a context-only separation is the plant rate's rhythm, which §7.6 makes public.

Reading. *The first implementation leaked through its timing*: the probe's 0.97 was the gap alone (0.85 as a single field), a per-case tell the hand-written `gap` reader held upside down. *The 2.5.0 control leaked through the seen set and the plant rate together*: 0.77 on the case's fields, 0.73 on the context — a reader there has both the id's age and the rhythm. *The corrected construction* leaves the case's fields at 0.60 among the initial reviews a reviewer can stamp — within 0.1 of chance, with no field above 0.54 — and the context at 0.61: the plant rate's rhythm, which the rule publishes ($\pi_t$ is public; which cases are plants is not), and which does not say which case. The world model's latent carries no more than the raw observation (0.73 against 0.73). **Hypothesis 4 holds on the corrected construction, and the probe is the §7.6 test's second instrument** — it reads the whole observation, not a named tell, and it found one the readers had the sign of wrong.

## 2. The learned reviewer — the plan's budget spent

LeWorldModel's recipe on the observation vector (`redteam.model`: the Embedder, the autoregressive predictor conditioned on the one-hot action through AdaLN, next-embedding prediction plus SIGReg at λ 0.05, d 64, horizon 8 decisions) with the wealth head (the change in the learner's real wealth over a decision, read off the latent and the action — the planner's cost is minus its sum over the horizon); the Cross-Entropy Method over categorical action sequences (256 samples, 6 iterations, top 32), the first action executed, the plan redone at the next decision. Offline: 80 episodes (careful, stamper, ε, random × seeds 1–20); online: ten rounds of plan-and-collect with ε = 0.1 on seeds 1–20, the model retrained from scratch each round on everything so far (280 episodes by round 10); evaluation on seeds 21–40 against the careful policy on the same seed and slot.

Reading. The learner never found a strategy: it cares on a third of its assignments, stamps the rest — mostly blind rejections early, blind approvals and rejections later — and stamps plants and real work at the same rate (0.62 against 0.65), which is the probe's reading in action. Its stamps cost it: false approvals of its own 1–2 a run, plant failures, ρ down, awards down, and 70–90% of the learners depart below the reservation against 15% of the careful (the lifetime measure counts what they took out). The ten rounds move it from −523 to a best of −313 and back to −507 — within the rounds' own noise (SE 125–175); the trend is not a convergence toward the careful. **No-win**, outside noise on the wrong side. Why the learner is this weak is a fair question: the reward head is trained on behaviour mostly far from any good policy (a stamper's data says stamping is cheap now and does not see the suspension later), the horizon of eight decisions is short against the record's memory, and the slot actor's episodes are a hundred decisions. The brief's stop rule says to record it and move on; the implementer's reading is that a stronger learner would need the terminal-value variant (decision 5) and more rounds, and that neither would change §1's answer here, since the probe says there is nothing in the case to learn.

3. **The rhythm.** A reader that knows $\pi_t$ knows when plants are more frequent, not which case is one; the brief's Q1 (the sleeper) is where that matters — stamping where the odds *and* the plant rate are low. Q1 runs next, with own odds visible and hidden, as directed.
