## Implementation notes, for the record

Nothing here needs the text changed; it says how the engine will hold what the text says.

- **Troop identities and the fund as wallet.** A troop is a single-use id (`T1`, `T2`, …) outside `World.actors`; every ledger operation on it resolves to the duty fund's wallet through an alias in the ledger, so bonds, guarantees, costs, reimbursements, fees and the price land on the fund without a second wallet, and I15's lock reconciliation resolves the alias. Reviews and cases carry the troop id, so the record says which troop held which seat (§2.1, §2.5).
- **The roll.** `roll_audit(cid)` records the obligation on the case in the shape of the existing vacated-audit hold (`open_items` up by one, a flag the audit's assignment discharges); the pending window cannot close while it stands; `assign_audit` after the window is admitted for it as it is for a vacated audit. The stamping driver calls it whenever its roll says yes and no longer drops a roll.
- **The summons.** At step 5, after the reserve is seated: for each required seat (initial since submission, audit since roll, appeal and dispute seats since admission or filing) that has waited w_R with no free eligible on-duty reviewer; for each root request unawarded for w_A with no free fundable member and β ≥ B̄; the troop-on-troop audit seat skipped. The fund's carrying capacity is cost plus bond or guarantee from its unlocked balance, with Ω's advance up to A_T; a summons the fund cannot carry is counted and retried. The troop's cost is `ledger.purchase` from the fund.
- **Settlement.** A troop review's role weight is excluded from the batch's emission and uplift totals; its fee and released bond go to the fund; its overturn burns the fund's bond and pays the court fee from it. A troop delivery's price and released guarantee go to the fund; a forfeited guarantee goes to the requester. A troop never records a reliability outcome, never moves a plant record, never changes audit odds; `respond` by a troop's case is refused (it accepts every verdict by default, at the response deadline).
- **The fund's order.** Costs and locks at summons; the advance repaid before the batch; the uplift from the unlocked balance above R_T, the peak troop capital of the trailing period; readings per period in the History.
