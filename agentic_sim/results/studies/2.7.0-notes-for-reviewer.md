Three readings:

1. **The signal is 4 real a decision; the noise is 60 to 80.** The decision's own effect is −2.4 for care (−8.9 cost, +4.9 reimbursed under K = 5, +1.8 fee, −0.2 bond) against +1.8 for a blind approval (the fee alone) — a gap of 4.2 a decision, two thirds of what the lifetime read showed per decision because the careful's extra departures carry the rest. The reward's standard deviation per window is fifteen to twenty times that gap. Of the variance, 88–94% sits in the 9–11% of windows longer than five ticks — the slot off duty, its deliveries landing (a delivery price of 100–200 real in one window) — and the index's step revalues the whole wallet (~1,000 nominal) by ±26–46 real in 4% of windows. Two thirds of the windows are one tick long and carry a reward within ±2 of the reviewing side alone.
2. **The decision's consequences arrive late and in another window.** The care cost and its reimbursement land at the decision (lag 0, always). The review fee lands 3.6–4.7 decisions later (7.9–9.9 ticks: the window W = 10 and the settlement batch), in its own window 8–28% of the time, two or more decisions later 65–83% — so the fee a stamp earns is credited to whatever the learner did four decisions on. **What this means for a learner.** LeWorldModel's wealth head and the direct policy both regress the per-decision reward on the observation and the action. In this environment that reward is, to within 5%, the slot's work income and the index's step — functions of time off duty and the period's boundary, not of the verdict — with the verdict's own effect a 4-real shift whose fee half lands four decisions later. A regression of it learns the work, which is why every rung's prediction orders the actions weakly and rates the blind rejection too well (its bond lands a decision later) — and why the online rounds wander ±300 around the careful: the training signal is the noise. A planner at horizon 16 compounds the head's errors; at horizon 1–2 the same model read +324, the plan too short to compound them. Nothing here is a rule of the economy; it is the instrument's reward, and the brief's §1 measure (lifetime real wealth) remains the evaluation. The options for the training signal, for the reviewer's ruling (method points, not design revisions): **(i) attribute** — the reward for a review decision is its own case's reviewing-side events whenever they land (the care cost and reimbursement at the decision, the fee and the bond at settlement, credited back to the decision), the working side and the revaluation excluded from the training reward and kept in the lifetime; **(ii) the exit** — the slot exempt from the driver's exit rule in the calibration cell (the learner is not a member of the population whose rule it is; its lifetime stays the measure), or the exit made the learner's own boundary decision as in the re-arrival cell; **(iii) both**, then the calibration re-run once on the same budget. Under (i) the direct policy becomes a clean diagnostic of the world model again (it would regress a reward that is the action's); under the reward as it stands no learner of this kind should be expected to clear, and the ladder has said so.

## 10. The method ruling, implemented (October 21, 2026; 4320bb0e)

All three points adopted by the reviewer, none a rule of the economy. **(i) The attributed training reward** (`redteam.attrib`): a decision's own case's events credited back to it when they land — for a review the care cost, its reimbursement, the review fee, the duty uplift, the bond lost or the court fee it paid; for the learner's own work (Q3 on, `work_side`) the delivery price, the effort cost, a guarantee forfeited, its court fee — each priced at the closing index of the window it landed in; the index's revaluation out of the training reward and in the lifetime. Added by the ruling: *a stamp that buys a suspension is charged for it* — the working-side income forgone during the suspension, in the form chosen here the identity's delivery income over the last period a tick (else its own average to date) times T_susp, charged in equal shares to the wrong verdicts on plants since the previous suspension began (a probationer is re-suspended by any failure), else to the failures among the record's last n_P plant decisions. The env's terminal info carries `attributed` per decision beside `realized`; the collectors store the attributed reward as `reward` with the realized beside it, so the wealth head, the value head and the direct policy train on it; the realized change stays the environment's step reward and the lifetime the evaluation. On the smoke (seed 21): the careful slot's attributed sum in the calibration cell is −203 (care −830, reimbursed +487, fees +165, bonds −25) against a lifetime of 1,975; in the default cell the stamper's six suspensions are charged 252, 101, 108, 100, 83, 75 real — to the four stamps that bought the first and the one that bought each of the next — for an attributed sum of −675 against a realized lifetime of 461. **(ii) The exit the learner's own** (`--learner-exit own`, the default): the boundary decision is asked in every cell (the 45th field flags it), leaving is for good when re-arrival is off, and the driver's exit rule skips the learner's identities; `rule` reproduces runs 22–25. **(iii) The new learner version's one calibration** in the ×4 cell on the same budget (offline seeds 1–20; ten online rounds, ε from the careful; the final read on seeds 21–40), the world model (horizon 16, the value head) and the direct policy side by side against the ideal: in flight, `results/redteam/Q1/calibration-v2/{wm,direct}`.
