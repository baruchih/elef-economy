# Agentic Economics — The Task, Verification, and Dispute Loop

## Design revision 17 — specification 2.9.0-rc.2: the plant takes the wrong step

**Project:** ELEF / Agentic Economics  
**Version:** 2.9.0-rc.2 — a release candidate, not yet in force. rc.1 (October 27) was the text as first sent; rc.2 settles the implementation review [I35]: **three corrections where the text met the engine** — (A) the planted approval's initial seat is **nobody's** from its submission to the orchestrator's verdict, the summons pass included, so the state's seat past $w_R$ never pre-empts the planted F; (B) the audit obligation is recorded at the verdict under the planted approval's **own mark**, not `market_audit_only`, and the seat is filled at a real audit seat's step; (C) the planted verdict is a **system review on the troop seat's road** — no actor, no fee, no adjudication, a fourth plant outcome `planted`, the bond destroyed with the pots and never burned — and **four facts** (the rate's base is the period's *real* approvals, kept by a credit; the lie rates read the *trailing* period; the lie's delay falls to a stated default where the real window is empty; the grounds are the population's one string); the ruling on `audit_odds_at_verdict` (October 27) under §2.6; and one conformance detail landed with the revision — a dispute's settlement discharges a standing audit obligation. On acceptance it becomes **2.9.0**, consolidated into one standalone text, and 2.8.0 moves to history. Semver as before: patch = wording, minor = one design revision under §19, major = a change of approach.  
**Prepared:** October 27, 2026 (rc.1–rc.2)  
**Supersedes:** 2.8.0 where this document speaks — what the orchestrator may plant (a verdict and a challenge beside the work), the rate at which it plants them, and the audit of a planted approval — and nowhere else.  
**Nature of this revision:** A **design revision** (minor, by §19), the fourth the learned red team has required and the second found at the level of a population. 2.8.0 put the orchestrator's known truth at every seat a real case can reach, and the self-play population answered by stamping the one seat the plants could not price — the audit (run 36: 77–98%), at every plant rate the dial can set (run 37: 82–98% at π 0.10 and 0.40, dial live or off, both learners) — and by siding with the challenger at the panels, which paid a lying actor's appeal of bad work 23 times in a hundred (run 37). The cause is in the plant's construction, not in its number: **a plant takes every step a real case takes at the real population's rate**, so it imitates an honest participant, and a seat that a case reaches only through someone's dishonesty — the audit seat through a wrong approval, the appeal panel's approve through a lying appeal — is priced only as much as the real population is dishonest, which is the quantity the plants exist to drive down. The better the first reviews, the cheaper the audit stamp (run 36's arithmetic, confirmed at both ends of the rate). This revision makes the plant take **the wrong step too**, at a rate the orchestrator sets: a bad plant with a wrong approval, sent to the audit; a plant actor that lies in an appeal and a plant requester that lies in a dispute, sent to the panels. What changes is where the orchestrator's known truth may appear and at what rate — not what any participant may do.  
**Design assumptions, unchanged.** Humans do not intervene; the orchestrator owns truth only for the cases it wrote.  
**Status:** Proposed. Accompanied by what required it (§1) and the runs that test it (§8).

## 1. Why: what runs 36 and 37 said

**The seat the population keeps (run 36, notes §54).** Under 2.8.0 the self-play reviewer population settles, from both starts and with both learners, at care 0.68–0.70 and stamps the audit seat 77–98% whatever the case; the standing false approvals 30–39 against the hand's 11.4 (from 86–98 under 2.7.0). The audit layer's overturn rate and the customers' dispute wins are back at an honest population's; the seat is not. The arithmetic (§55): the share of audit stamps that fail is π β_P s / [π(1 − β_P + β_P s) + (1 − π) f_R] — 0.054 at π 0.21 and s 0.25 — against the first review's β_P π = 0.16, which does not depend on s; the factor is s, the first review's own stamp rate on plants. **The plants reach the audit seat only through a first reviewer who let a bad plant pass.**

**The rate is not the lever (run 37, §59–§60).** With π pinned at its floor 0.10 and at its cap 0.40, dial live or off, the audit seat is stamped 82–98% by both learners; with the dial live the standing false approvals do not move with the rate (3.45× and 3.60× the hand's against the base's 3.4×); with it off they double, because m_t's odds on the first review's approvals are what bound the dishonest basin. F̂ at the audit seat reads 0.85 beside the dial's 0.21 — and feeding it to the dial would raise odds the audit stamper does not carry: the audit stamp is a verdict on another reviewer's approval. **No rate of honest-shaped plants prices the seat.**

**Why the record cannot reach it.** 1.2.0's plant record is a rate over a reviewer's trailing n_P = 20 bad plants, pooled over every seat it sits. A member that cares at its ~70 initial seats a run meets ~11 bad plants there and keeps its record clean; at its ~12 audit seats a run it meets under one bad plant (14% of audit seats are plants, 43% of those bad), and stamping every one moves â by a twentieth. **A record pooled over seats lets a member buy its stamps at a rare seat with its care at a common one** — and the audit seat is rare in bad plants exactly because the common seat is careful. The price reaches the audit seat only when the bad plants a member meets there are of the order of the bad plants it meets at the first review; the construction cannot supply that count, since it depends on s.

**The challenger is always right (run 37, §59–§60).** With real challengers lying at 0.1, the population cares at the dispute seat (where 42% of disputes are lies) and keeps its blind approve at the appeal seat (26%): a lying actor's appeal of truly bad work is paid 23–31 times in a hundred against the hand's 2. Every challenge the plant instrument files is truthful by 2.8.0's principle — the plant actor appeals only a good plant's N, the plant requester disputes only a bad plant's F, each at the real rate — so a panelist that sides with the challenger is never failed by a plant.

**What this revision does not do.** The planted approval is not the state's visible seat (an auditor that cares on state-approved cases and stamps on member-approved ones is captured by half); the author of a verdict is not on the auditor's surface today (run 37, §62: the ReviewView and the learner's observation carry nothing of it), and this revision keeps it so. The record is not split by seat: once the count at the audit seat is the dial's, the pooled record prices the seat as it prices the first review, and a split would only make the rare seats' records slow to determine. The audit of a planted approval is not drawn from the real approvals' odds: the orchestrator sends its planted approval to the seat it planted it for.

## 2. The rules

### 2.1 The principle, extended

**Rule.** 2.6.0's and 2.8.0's principle stands — *a plant takes every step a real case takes, in the pass a real case takes it, at a drawn position, with parameters drawn from the trailing real cases'* — for every **honest** step. For the **wrong** steps — the steps that alone bring a case to a seat where a stamp is otherwise cheap — the plant takes them **at a rate the orchestrator sets**, not at the real population's: the wrong approval of a bad plant (§2.2) and the lying challenge of a plant (§2.3). The wrong step is taken in the real pass at a drawn position, with its timing and parameters drawn from the real population's as every plant step is; only its *rate* is the orchestrator's. The orchestrator's known truth is thereby present at every seat **at a rate the population does not set**.

### 2.2 The planted approval

**Rule.** Beside the dial's plants, the orchestrator posts **planted approvals**: bad plants, marked as such at their posting, that live a plant's life to their submission (posted into the market under a fresh requester alias, awarded to a fresh actor alias when the market's selection reports or at the state's price past $w_A$, submitted at a drawn time — 2.6.x and 2.8.0 as they stand) and receive their **initial verdict from the orchestrator itself: F, wrong on purpose.** From its submission to that verdict **the planted approval's initial seat is nobody's**: no member is seated on it and the summons pass passes it by (the seat past $w_R$ is not the state's here, as it is on every other plant under 2.8.0 (C) — a state's N on it would pre-empt the planted F, and a state's shape on it would be the half-capture §1 names); its wait counts as a waiting case's for the reserve's sizing. The verdict is the orchestrator's own operation, given in the review pass at a drawn position among the tick's real initial verdicts when its drawn delay has elapsed — the seating gap drawn from the trailing real roots' (0, 1 or 2 ticks in run 24's world) and the review's own tick, so that its times have a real review's shape. The verdict is recorded as a **system review** on the road the troop's seat takes (§2.5): a review id, the verdict F, a bond, a seating and a verdict time, under a fresh **plant reviewer alias** (`plant_rev:`, the class of the plant actor's and requester's aliases, 2.6.0 §2.2); no actor stands behind it, no fee is paid for it, it is **not adjudicated** (it is the orchestrator's own: a fourth plant outcome, *planted*, beside stood, failed and the state's), and the dial's clock and its readings do not count it.

**Rule — the audit.** A planted approval's pending F is **audited with certainty**: the case carries its own mark, its live audit odds are 1, and the obligation is **recorded at the verdict** — as I25 records a troop's own delivery's obligation, and nothing more of that path — counted in the period's approvals and the audit demand at 1 as a real approval is at its odds. The audit seat is filled at the step a real audit seat is filled: a free eligible member, or past $w_R$ the orchestrator's own verdict through the plant branch 2.8.0 (C) gave the pass. The auditor's verdict is **adjudicated by the plant's truth** (2.8.0 §2.2): a confirmation is a plant failure, a rejection stands; a rejection opens the third seat as an audit's disagreement does on a real case, adjudicated by truth. The plant requester disputes the planted approval at the real requesters' rate, as it disputes any bad plant's F (2.8.0): where the real requesters dispute first, the planted approval meets the dispute panel and not the audit, as a real wrong approval would, and **the dispute's settlement discharges the standing obligation** (§4).

**Rule — the rate.** Planted approvals are posted at $\pi^A_t = \pi^A_0\, m^A_t$ **per real pending approval** — the period's real roots' initial Fs, which the orchestrator credits as they are given (a planted-approval credit beside the plant credit, since the period's approvals count the plants' Fs too) — with $m^A_t$ a multiplier of the dial's kind (§2.4) answering $\hat F^A_t$, the share of planted approvals **confirmed at the audit seat** in the period among those adjudicated there, against $F^*$. In an honest population $\hat F^A$ sits under $F^*$ and $\pi^A$ at its floor; in a population that stamps the audit seat the multiplier rises until the seat is priced or the cap is reached.

**Rule — what the planted approval is for.** It supplies the audit seat with bad approved work at the orchestrator's rate, so that the bad plants a member meets at its audit seats are of the order of the bad plants it meets at its first reviews, and the pooled record prices the audit stamp as it prices the first review's — through 1.2.0 §2.2 unchanged: $\hat a$ over the trailing $n_P$ bad plants, $\theta_{\text{panel}}$ for the audit and panel seats, $\theta_{\text{duty}}$ for duty. The organic path — a bad plant wrongly approved by a member and audited at that member's odds — continues as 2.8.0 has it; the first reviewer's odds remain the real price of its stamp.

### 2.3 The planted lie

**Rule.** Beside the plant's truthful challenges (2.8.0: the plant actor appeals a good plant's N and the plant requester disputes a bad plant's F, at the real rates), the orchestrator files **planted lies**: the plant actor **appeals a bad plant's N**, and the plant requester **disputes a good plant's F**, each at a rate the orchestrator sets. A planted lie takes the real challenge's path — filed in the pass at a drawn position, at a delay drawn from the trailing real challenges' window of the kind, truthful and lying alike, and **where that window is empty at the stated default** (Appendix A: 0 ticks, the delay every real requester and actor has shown in every standing cell); its grounds the population's own — in every standing cell one string per kind, which the panel's view carries and the planted lie carries too; the court fee minted to the alias and destroyed at filing (2.8.0 (A)); two fresh reviewers; the panel's verdicts **adjudicated by truth**: a panelist that sides with the lie (F on the bad plant's appeal; N on the good plant's dispute) is a plant failure, a panelist that holds the truth stands; the case resolves by truth with no second overturn and no ruling (2.8.0 §2.2). The first reviewer, whose verdict the lie contests, is not adjudicated again. The windows the plant's *truthful* challenges draw from read the real truthful challenges alone, as 2.8.0 rules. A lying appeal spends the bad plant's N — the plant actor's acceptance today — for a panel; that is its cost.

**Rule — the rate.** Planted lying appeals at $\pi^{La}_t = \pi^{La}_0\, m^{La}_t$ **per real appeal** and planted lying disputes at $\pi^{Ld}_t = \pi^{Ld}_0\, m^{Ld}_t$ **per real dispute of the trailing closed period** (the count the orchestrator has when it files), each with a **floor of two a period** — applied when the trailing count is zero or the product is under two — so that a panel kind no real challenger uses is still tested; $m^{La}$ answers $\hat F^{La}$ (the share of planted lying appeals a panelist sided with) and $m^{Ld}$ answers $\hat F^{Ld}$ (the dispute's), against $F^*$. Where no real challenger uses a panel kind, the floor makes that panel a seat only plants reach, and a panelist that learns to care there is right by truth — stated, and read in §8.4 as the base rate it is.

**Rule — what the planted lie is for.** The truthful planted challenge (2.8.0) prices the panel's *reject of a truthful appeal* and *approve of a truthful dispute*; the planted lie prices the panel's *approve of a lying appeal* and *reject of a lying dispute* — the blind siding with the challenger — at a rate the real liars do not set.

### 2.4 The dial per seat

**Rule.** The dial of 1.2.0 §2.3 is read **per planted wrong step**: one multiplier each — $m^A_t$, $m^{La}_t$, $m^{Ld}_t$ — stepping as $m_t$ steps (within $[0.9, 2]$ a period, $1 \le m \le m_{\max}$), each on its own seat's reading against the one target $F^*$, and each moving **its rate alone** — not the odds, not $X_t$, not $\omega$, which answer $m_t$ as before: the audit stamper's price is its record, not its odds. The plant rate $\pi_t$ and its multiplier $m_t$ on the initial adjudications unchanged. A multiplier whose reading is undefined in a period (no planted step of its kind adjudicated) does not move. The dial's clock — the period's length and $\hat F$ over the initial adjudications — counts initial adjudications only, as 2.8.0 has it; the planted verdict is not one and never reaches the adjudication.

### 2.5 The money, and the system reviewer

**Rule.** The planted verdict's review is a **system review** on the road the troop's seat already takes: built directly, no actor touched, its reviewer a third system identity — the plant reviewer alias, an entry in the ledger's aliases to **a plant reviewer wallet of the bank's** (beside the plant actor's and requester's), so its mint and destruction read apart in the cost of truth — its bond **minted to the alias and locked**, its cost nil. Everywhere the engine asks whether a reviewer is a troop it now asks whether it is a *system reviewer* (a troop or a plant reviewer alias), and the alias behaves as a troop does — no actor, no record, no emission, no uplift, no place in the rotation — with two differences: **no fee** (a troop is paid its fixed fee; the alias is paid nothing) and **no adjudication** (the review's outcome is *planted*; I20 exempts it; a troop on a plant stays forbidden). At the case's closure the review settles as stood without fee, its bond **released to the alias's wallet and destroyed with the pots' remainder** — never burned as a forfeit, which is a real reviewer's penalty and a different sink in the bank's record. The planted lie's court fee is minted to the alias and destroyed at filing. The planted approval's audit seat, third seat and panel seats, and the planted lie's panels, are paid their fee and reimbursed their cost from the plant's pots, topped up at each seat's assignment (2.8.0 (A)); forfeited bonds are burned. **Nothing minted for a planted approval or a planted lie reaches Ω, a real actor or a real requester except as a reviewer's fee for a seat it held** — I42, extended.

### 2.6 The surfaces

**Rule.** The reviewer's view (§14, 1.2.0) carries nothing of a verdict's author — not its name, not its kind, not its odds — and this revision keeps it so: on the surface a reviewer's policy reads, a planted approval is a pending F like any other, equal in shape to a real pending F. On the public record, the planted approval's review is shaped as a real review (an id, a case, a role, a seating and a verdict time, a bond, the verdict F) under an alias of the known class; the public surface carries **nothing the engine writes about a plant before its reveal** — run 37's ruling on the case log, extended to the class the implementer listed and landed (`e32a8db9`: the case's plant fields and grade at a real root's values, the review's plant outcome and truth masked, the ledger's plant writes without case or note) — and the orchestrator's own instrument and the declared readers reach them by the orchestrator's road, not the public one. **`audit_odds_at_verdict` joins the masked fields**: for a planted approval the public surface shows it at a value drawn once at the verdict from the trailing real approvals' recorded odds and stored, until the reveal, and the true 1 after; the roll reads the engine's 1 by the orchestrator's road, not the public field; a troop's own-delivery approval at 1 is a real case and stays as it is. The alias names remain the disguise's stated boundary (2.6.0 §2.2): on the view a planted approval is distinguishable by its reviewer's name and by nothing else; on the convention's surface by nothing.

## 3. Hypotheses

1. **The audit seat is priced and the population cares there.** On the self-play chains of run 36 (the careful basin, the stamper basin; disputes off), the audit seat's care rises from 2–23% toward the first review's (above 0.6), from both starts and with both learners; the standing false approvals fall **within a factor of two of the hand's** — 2.8.0's unmet clause; $m^A$ rises to where $\hat F^A \le F^*$ holds and the cost of truth rises with it; the first review's care and the panels' do not fall. The seat's price is read in the record: the bad plants a member meets at its audit seats reach the order of its first reviews', and audit-stampers leave the panels (θ_panel) in proportion to their stamps. This is a disputes-off hypothesis: under truthful requesters disputing first, the planted approval meets the dispute panel and the audit seat sees it only through what the requesters leave.
2. **The lying challenger is no longer paid.** On the liar chain of run 37 ((c): real challengers lying at 0.1), the appeal seat's blind approve falls and a lying actor's appeal of bad work is paid at the hand's rate (~2%, from 23–31%); the lying requester's dispute at the hand's (~1.7%, from 4.4%); the truthful challenges won as before (94–98%); the population's care at the panels rises rather than its blind siding.
3. **The honest populations are unmoved and pay little.** In the four hand cells of run 37 (§8.3), honesty, P_t, the members' wealth and the plants per period within noise; $\pi^A$ and the lie rates at their floors ($\hat F$ under $F^*$ at every seat); the cost of truth up by the floor's plants and panels — a few percent (the audit seats +2.5% at the floor), against 2.8.0's +18–72%.
4. **No tell.** The probe at the audit seat reads at chance (the gap raw − context within two SE of zero, as run 37 read it), and on the planted approvals against the organic wrong approvals at that seat likewise; at the panels the question's base rate only (1.0 at a panel kind only plants reach, stated); the planted approval's and the planted lie's stages — the seating gap, the verdict's tick, the filing delay, the audit's wait — match the real ones period-matched; the stamp rates plant/real by seat within the run 36 figures.
5. **Each rule alone.** (A) alone closes the audit seat and leaves the appeal seat's blind approve; (B) alone closes the panels and leaves the audit seat; together both. The dishonest basin's bound is the dial's odds as run 37 read it; with $m^A$ at its cap the population's remaining stamps are at the price the rules set.

## 4. What does not change, and one conformance detail

Everything in 2.8.0: the plant's life to its submission, the truthful planted challenges, the audit of an organic wrong approval at the first reviewer's odds, the third seat, the resubmission, the state's seat past $w_R$ on every plant but the planted approval's initial seat, the adjudication table, the markers at closure, the money of 2.8.0 (A), the dial's clock. The plant record (1.2.0 §2.2) — its window, its thresholds, its pooling over seats — unchanged: this revision changes what the record *sees* at the rare seats, not how it is read. The reviewer's view unchanged. No participant may do anything it could not do under 2.8.0; the state's visible seats are as they were; troops never sit on a plant.

**One conformance detail, landed with the revision.** A case whose audit obligation stands when its dispute is settled has had its approval judged by two fresh reviewers; the obligation is **discharged at the dispute's settlement**, whichever way the panel found. The rulebook's one round per case is read so; the engine today leaves a standing obligation on a disputed-and-lost real case undischarged, which has not happened in any standing cell (the requesters dispute in the pass before the roll) and which a planted approval would meet every time with disputes on. The rules do not move; a regression carries it.

## 6. Worked example

Run 24's world, tick 400, the dial at π 0.21, $m^A$ risen to 3 (π^A 0.06). The period's real approvals ~150; nine planted approvals are posted: bad work under fresh requester aliases, awarded to actor aliases in the market's pass, submitted at drawn times. One of them, P, submits at tick 412; its initial seat is nobody's — the seating step offers it to no member and the summons pass passes it by; its drawn seating gap is 1. Tick 413: the review is seated under `plant_rev:3f…` with a bond of $G^V$ minted and locked; tick 414, in the review pass, at the 7th position among the tick's 11 real initial verdicts, the orchestrator records F on P; P is pending with its window, its audit obligation recorded at odds 1 under its mark; the dial's clock does not move and no plant is adjudicated. Tick 416: the audit seat is filled by member M, free and eligible; M's view shows a pending F with its gap, ceiling and bond — nothing of the author; the public record shows the review under the alias and `audit_odds_at_verdict` at the drawn 0.31. M confirms blind: a plant failure — M's bond burned, its record −1 (its 4th bad plant approved of its trailing 20: $\hat a = 5/22 = 0.23 > \theta_{\text{panel}}$ — M leaves the panels at the next assignment), its odds doubled; P resolves by truth: final N at $t_0 + W$; the alias's bond released and destroyed with the pots' remainder, the reveal showing the review's outcome *planted* and the odds 1. Had M rejected, M's verdict stands (record +1) and the third seat opens as on a real case, adjudicated by truth. Had P's requester disputed first (the real rate), the dispute's panel would have judged it and the obligation been discharged at the settlement. Beside it, tick 420: a bad plant Q answered N by careful member K; the plant actor files a planted lying appeal with the population's grounds (D minted to the alias, destroyed), two fresh panelists; one approves blind — a plant failure — one rejects — stood; Q resolves by truth, K not adjudicated again.

## Closing statement
