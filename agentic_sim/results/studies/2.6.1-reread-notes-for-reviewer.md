## 1. What went in

- **The ceilings enter the window as posted.** `post_root` appends the real root's ceiling to the rolling window at its posting (awarded or
  not; a later raise does not rewrite the entry); the award no longer does. The window's reading (the last k real roots, k the trailing
  period's count) is unchanged; the submission draw reads the same window (confirmed).
- **The plant requester raises.** `raise_ceiling` admits a plant in the market: the difference is minted as the posting was (`mint_plant`; no ω
  — a plant pays no fee), locked under the posting lock and destroyed with the rest at the plant's closure; `plants_raised` is read in
  `dial()`. The driver's `raise_below_market` — the population's requesters' step, in the posting pass before the awards — covers the plants in
  the working set with the same rule and timing (`--ceiling-policy`, `--patient-ticks`), so the plant takes the step in the same pass, at the
  same moment, as the real roots taking it. One asymmetry stays and is declared: a requester's raise is refused when its wallet cannot lock
  the difference; the plant's is minted and never refused.
- **The principle** (Appendix S) and run 24's acceptance recorded; `test_v26` 19 (the window as posted, the plant's raise, a created-submitted
  plant's raise refused); goldens re-pinned once more; the probe names its episodes (the four offline policies by default, recorded in its
  output).

The plant requester raises as often as the population's requesters now (36 % against 34 %), and the plant's tail to w_A is gone — the raise

is now the *younger* of the two at submission by a little (seated later 16.5 % against 13.1 %), the reverse of run 24's sign and a third of
its size.
