These are the six readings and the two reads ordered in brief A1's addendum 9 (I3, I4). All of it is in the stressed world: harness options, off in every run of record. No rule is changed and nothing is drafted. "Claim" is the stressed world's fourth action: take the allowance for a check and approve without looking. "Skip" and "claim" mean the same. The benefit of a claim is the effort it saves, times the temptation; it is off the ledger.*

## In plain words

1. **A little skipping does not pay where a failure costs the member its place.** A careful member that skips 1 review in 10 ends 88 to 154 real behind the careful, and one that skips 3 in 10 ends 307 to 422 behind. Each skip costs 13 to 33 real of lifetime against a saving of about 2.5, and it is the first review that carries the price.
2. **At the member's audit seat alone it is about level.** A member that skips 3 audit seats in 10 loses about half its audit seats and nothing else, and ends 56 ± 48 real behind. A skip there costs about a third of what it costs at the first review, and the read cannot tell that from nothing. It is the seat the stake's table was built for.
3. **A fresh start does not pay.** A skipper that leaves when it is suspended and comes back under new names ends 449 to 530 real behind a careful member among careful members, and 839 to 1,028 behind in a mixed population. Its new names are never admitted, and carry out almost nothing.
4. **How much more tempting skipping would have to be.** A lone skipper among careful members that stays, or returns, draws level where skipping is worth about three times the cost of the check (2.5 to 3.5). The seven to eight times of the last record held for skippers that leave when suspended, as the simulation's exit rule has it. This is the reviewer's arithmetic on the note's figures; the implementer is asked to confirm it.
5. **Tests and a higher fee cost careful members nothing in real terms.** That either pays them is not shown.
6. **The reviewer's misses.** The condition that would refute the dose was written without naming the seat, and at one seat it is met: by its letter the expectation is refuted there. Yesterday's sentence that the record does the work "at the margin" was too wide. Three smaller ones are in section 4.
7. **Next:** the member's audit seat under the stakes, read by the same dose, on more seeds. And the learner with a horizon: Baruchi ruled on November 13 that it is to be built.

## 5. What it means

- **Careful is the best life against a fresh start.** A new name begins with nothing but a loan, sees only reviews that are all checked, and gets no job until it passes. A skipper never passes.
- **Careful is the best life against a little skipping wherever a failure costs the member its place.** That is the first review. A skip there is paid in suspensions and in the place, not in the seat's own stake.
- **Where a failure costs only the seat, the seat has to be worth a great deal, or it is weak.** That is the member's audit seat. It is the seat every learned chain skipped most, the seat the stake's table was built for, and the seat a learning population still skipped one time in four to one in eight under heavy stakes. The dose shows why: a failure there takes the seat and leaves the place.
- **So "what stops a skipper is losing its place" is true where the place is what it loses.** The last record's sentence, that for an agent that counts its future the record does the work at the margin, holds at the first review and not at the member's audit seat.
- **The margin is about three, not seven.** A lone skipper among careful members that stays or returns is clearly behind at a temptation of 1 and draws level near 3. Under arm 4 at a temptation of 2 it is already inside the error.
- **A careful population tests a lone skipper least.** The dial follows the population's failures, so where nearly all are careful few plants come. This is why the margin among the careful is half that in the mixed population.
- **For the staged loop.** Baruchi's note on decision 4, that a member that does not review well takes no new jobs, is the lever that works here. The premium seat is the seat it does not yet reach: if a failed planted task there costs only the premium seat, it is this simulation's audit seat again. Either the seat pays enough that losing it hurts, or a failure there costs the member's jobs too. The next read tests the first. And admission as the simulation has it holds against a skipper that returns: no job before a newcomer has passed reviews that are all checked, and whatever it is advanced is a loan.
- **The orchestrator's audit.** Under it there is no weak seat, and every dose is behind. It also leaves a member less to lose, and the lone skipper's margin is lowest there. Both are recorded; the recommendation Baruchi did not adopt is not reopened.

## 6. Limits

- The audit seat's dose is one world of twenty seeds. Its point figure is a small loss; its error covers nothing and three times as much.
- The returning name is one conduct: it claims at every seat. One that reviews carefully until it is admitted and skips afterwards is the entry test's skipper with a delay, and was not run as such.
- The temptation at which a skipper draws level is arithmetic on two paired gaps, with errors of half a unit or more.
- What a slot earns after it leaves is counted as nothing in every lifetime here. The implementer is asked what the population's exit rule assumes.
- Whether a skipper is ahead of the careful until it is first caught is not read. If it is, a member that means to leave anyway has nothing to lose by skipping at the end.
- A harness construction; scripted policies; every learner still credits one decision at a time.
